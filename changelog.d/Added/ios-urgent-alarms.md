- **Urgent reminders ring as alarms on iPhone.** Choose **Urgent** in a reminder's menu and, in the iOS app on iOS 26 or later, it rings as an alarm over silent mode and Focus until you tap **Stop** or **Snooze 10 min**. iOS asks to allow alarms with your first urgent reminder; without that, on older iOS and everywhere else, an urgent reminder is an ordinary reminder. The flag syncs, so a reminder marked urgent on your computer rings on your iPhone ([#1954](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/1954)).
