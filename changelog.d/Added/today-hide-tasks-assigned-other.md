- **Today can hide tasks assigned to other people.** Today's menu offers **Hide tasks assigned to others** once a shared project has someone else in it. It is off until you turn it on. While it is on, Today, its count, the icon badge, the briefing and the home-screen widget leave out a shared task assigned to someone else. Tasks with no assignee, tasks assigned to you, Inbox and projects this device does not know as shared stay. Upcoming, including when it starts with Today, is unchanged. Filters can say `assigned`, `unassigned`, `assigned: me` or `assigned: "Name"`. ([#827](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/827))
