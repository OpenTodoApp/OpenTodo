- **The Notes page is a home for your notes.** Type in the search field and the first row is **New note "…"**: when nothing matches, Enter creates the note with that title and puts the caret in its text (in the Project filter's project, with one Undo), and when notes match, Enter still opens the best match. Pin a standalone note from its menu, the palette or `p` on its row: pinned notes are listed first under **Pinned**, the others under **Recent**, and they also show in the sidebar's Favourites. Pins are personal and sync as a favourite of the kind `note`, which 1.0 clients keep unchanged; the MCP tool `set_favourite` accepts `kind: "note"`. The Source filter gains **Unlinked**, the standalone notes nothing links to, worked out on the device.
