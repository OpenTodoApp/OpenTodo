- **Add tasks from the Android notification shade.** Turn on **Settings → Capture → Quick add → Quick add notification** in the Android app, and a silent notification stays in the shade. Tap **Add task**, type "Call the plumber tomorrow 9am #Home" and send it without leaving the app you're in: the task is saved the way quick add reads it, in the Inbox unless you name a project, and syncs when the phone is online. Tap the notification itself to open quick add. It's off until you turn it on, per phone, and it comes back after a restart ([#2046](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/2046)).
