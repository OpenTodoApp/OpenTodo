- **Undo and redo more than the last step.** Ctrl/⌘+Z now walks back through your recent actions and Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z (or Ctrl+Y) brings them back, no longer only while the undo toast is showing; Undo and Redo are in the command palette too, each named for its step ("Undo: Complete 2 tasks"), and the message afterwards says what was undone or redone.
