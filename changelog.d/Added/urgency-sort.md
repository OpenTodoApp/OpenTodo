- **Order a list by urgency.** **Display options → Order by → Urgency** on a project, a label page, the Inbox or a saved view puts what needs you first at the top: one score per task from its date, deadline, priority, the tasks waiting for it, its age and a running timer, lowered while it is blocked, starts later or is parked in Someday. Each row shows its score; Tab to it or open **Why here?** in the task's menu for the breakdown. The score is worked out on your device, offline and on encrypted accounts too, and the assistant tools (`list_tasks` with `sort: "urgency"`) and `ot list --sort urgency` order the same way. Older versions show the list's default order.
