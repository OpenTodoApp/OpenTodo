- **The phone's tab bar follows the sidebar order.** Inbox, Today and Upcoming on a phone use the order from **Settings → Appearance → Sidebar destinations**, the same order as the sidebar. Search stays last, and Calendar and the other destinations stay in the drawer. Someone who never reordered still sees Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Search. The order is the one already saved with the account, so it is right offline and follows the other devices after sync. ([#873](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/873))
