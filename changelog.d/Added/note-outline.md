- **Outline of a long note.** A task note, project note or standalone note with three or more headings shows its outline: a column beside a wide note that marks where you are, or an **Outline** button in the note's corner. Choose a heading to go to it, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd>) in the note, then the arrows and <kbd>Enter</kbd>; <kbd>Esc</kbd> returns to the note.
