- **Urgent reminders ring as alarms on Android.** A reminder marked **Urgent** turns the screen on over the lock screen and plays the alarm sound, also with the ringer silent, until you tap **Done**, **Snooze 10 min** or **Dismiss** (swiping it away or opening the notifications only silences it). The first time you turn **Urgent** on, Android asks to allow exact alarms and, on Android 14 and later, full-screen notifications; without them the alarm can be a few minutes late or rings as a notification. Do Not Disturb treats it as an alarm ([#2023](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/2023)).
