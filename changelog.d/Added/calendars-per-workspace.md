- **Calendars per workspace.** The **Calendars** button of Day, Week, Plan, Month and Year opens a menu: **Show calendar events** still hides every calendar on this device, and below it a checkbox per calendar hides it in the current workspace only, so a work calendar can stay out of Personal. The choice syncs to your other devices, new calendars show everywhere, and hidden calendars still count as busy for free time ([#1828](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/1828)).
