- **Push to calendar apps.** Calendar apps connected to `/dav/` no longer have to poll: with **Push to calendar apps** on (Settings → Integrations → Calendar apps, off by default, `OPENTODO_CALDAV_PUSH` presets it), apps that support WebDAV-Push, such as DAVx⁵, sync within about a minute of a change on any device. Each push is encrypted for the app and names no task, list or person; it goes to the push service the app chose, at most once per subscription every 30 seconds ([#1585](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/1585)).
