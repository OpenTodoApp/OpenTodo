- **The server is ready for the OpenTodo app's sign-in.** The coming native app signs in through your phone's own sign-in sheet: your server's sign-in page opens there (password, two-factor, passkeys and single sign-on all work), and if that browser is signed in already you only confirm "Sign in to the OpenTodo app on iPhone?". The app gets its own tokens through an authorization code with PKCE, and its refresh token changes on every use; a used one coming back signs the app out. Signed-in apps appear with your sessions in Settings → Account, where you can sign one out, and Sign out everywhere, a password change or deactivation sign them out too. `GET /api/v1/health` says `app_sign_in: true` so the app can tell. See [App sign-in](docs/api.md#app-sign-in).
