- **The assistant's answers show your tasks.** Where it saves reading, an answer shows the tasks it talks about as rows you can tick off, a day or week as planned with its total, places on the map, a note, a project's outline, or a choice you answer with one click, all read from your device, so they stay current and work offline. Tasks it names are links. A proposal that moves work within a week reads as a calendar (a new project as an outline, places as a map) with **List** one switch away, and its titles, dates and priority can be edited before you apply. Under each answer: the steps it took, **Copy answer**, **Regenerate** and **Edit question**; the conversation follows the answer with **Jump to latest**. The same works on encrypted accounts with the assistant on the device; MCP clients are unchanged.
