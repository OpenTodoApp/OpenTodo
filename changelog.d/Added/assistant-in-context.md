- **The assistant where you work.** A task's **⋯** menu offers **Rewrite this** (a clearer title and description) and **Find a slot** (a day and time in your free time this week that fits the task), a project's menu **What's next?** (its next actions with the board or timeline) and Today's menu **Brief me** (the agenda, deadlines and how you are doing). Each opens the assistant with its skill and subject and sends at once, only while the assistant is available. The four skills are MCP prompts too.
