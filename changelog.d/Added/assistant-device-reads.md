- **The assistant on encrypted accounts reads your notes and your time.** With the assistant running on your device, it can now list, search and read your notes, list your tracked time with totals, and read your calendar frames and slots, all from your device's decrypted copy, so it answers "where did my time go this week?" or "what's in my notes about the move?" offline. Projects kept away from assistants stay out, and the list of what may be sent names notes, tracked time, frames and slots. On the server, `list_slots` no longer shows tasks of projects kept away from assistants.
