- **Away.** Going on holiday, or ill for a few days? **Away…** in Today's menu or the command palette skips your repeating tasks' occurrences from one day to another, also after the fact, in one batch with one Undo. Habit streaks survive, a task that repeats after completion moves to the day after, and reminders and the email digest pause on every device and channel while you are away (dropped, not delivered later). Today reads *Away until Friday*, and **End now** comes back early. `POST` and `DELETE /api/v1/away` and `ot away` do the same from scripts ([Away](docs/away.md)).
