- **Show tasks assigned to others.** In a shared workspace, the menu of Today, Upcoming, Anytime and Someday has a checkable **Show tasks assigned to others**: checked, those lists also show other people's tasks, and the check shows that it is on. The choice syncs to your other devices; counts, the badge and your Today slots still count only your own tasks ([#1979](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/1979)).
