- **Focus on every device.** A Focus session now syncs to your other devices: start it on the laptop and the phone shows the same task and time left, and you can pause, resume, take a break or finish it on either, offline too. The later change wins when two devices change it at once, and only the device where you last started, paused or resumed it plays the sound or shows the notification ([#1943](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/1943)).
