- **Search operators for notes.** `OR` in capitals finds either word (`ferry OR train`), `title:` and `text:` look in one field (`title:wifi`, `text:"oat milk"`), and `changed:2026-10-01..2026-10-31` keeps notes changed within those days, with This month and Last month among the suggestions. The Notes page, `ot notes search` and the assistants' `search_notes` read queries the same way; a query without operators finds what it found before.
