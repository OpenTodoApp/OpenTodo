- **Display options on Today, Upcoming and the Inbox.** The Display options button of projects and labels is now in the header of the three built-in lists. Today can be grouped by project, priority or label instead of the time of day, with Earlier still first and Blocked, Waiting for a slot and Done today still at the end; Upcoming orders each day by priority, title or another order; the Inbox groups by due date, priority or label and orders by any of them. Today and Upcoming can **Nest subtasks** under a parent in the same group, and **Reset** puts a view back as it was. The keys `r g`, `r o` and `r r` work there too, with `r g t` for Time of day on Today. The choices are thirteen synced `user_prefs` fields; an older device keeps showing the defaults, and the Today limit fills its slots as before (#872, #1089, #1108).
