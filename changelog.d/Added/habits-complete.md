- **Habits feel like a habits app, on a phone too.** With the phone screens on, Habits has a strip of the last seven days with each day's progress, a ring under your thumb for every habit and its week beside it: choose yesterday to fix a day you forgot, hold a habit for the tiny version, Skip this day, a note, Open or Edit. Everywhere, the screen says how today is going ("3 of 5 today"), a second tap on a kept habit takes the check-in back, **Skip this day** is in each habit's menu, the habit form has **Remind me** for a daily reminder at a time, and a habit's page shows its month in its own colour and its last 53 weeks as a heatmap. With Habits off, nothing of it shows.
