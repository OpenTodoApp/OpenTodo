- **Links to other apps.** Settings → Integrations → Links to other apps lists the URL schemes whose links open in other apps on your devices (`zotero`, `mid`, `obsidian:open`…), empty by default. Links such as `zotero://select/items/ABC` in notes, comments and a task's origin are kept as written and open for you only when your own list allows them, after a confirmation the first time on each device; `javascript:`, `data:`, `file:` and a few others never open. A calendar app's VTODO `URL` with such a link becomes the task's origin, `ot add --origin-url` takes one, and the Android and iOS apps hand them to the system through the new `app-links` capability ([Links to other apps](docs/app-links.md)).
