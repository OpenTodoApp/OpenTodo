- **The assistant answers questions about time with the app's own views.** "Where did my time go this week?" shows your tracked time as bars per project or per day with the totals, "When am I free for two hours?" shows each day's free stretches within your working day, between calendar events, frames, slots and timed tasks, and "What's due before Friday?" lists the deadlines in date order with their countdowns, all read from your device. The new read tool `find_free_time` gives the same free stretches to MCP clients, such as Claude Desktop, and to the assistant on an encrypted account's device; it reads only times, never an event's title.
