- **See what an item is connected to.** **Show connections** in the menu of a note, task or project with a link, the graph button of its Linked references, or the command palette opens a calm graph with the item in the middle, what it links with on an inner ring and their links on an outer ring (at most 24 and 48, the most recently changed, with a "+n" for the rest). Click or tap an item to move it to the middle and click the middle to open it; from the keyboard the arrow keys move between items, Space moves one to the middle, Enter opens it, Backspace goes back and Esc closes. **List** shows the same as Links and Linked from and is remembered on the device; it comes first on a phone when the graph does not fit. The picture is the same every time, worked out on your device from the link index, offline and with end-to-end encryption, and loaded only on first use.
