- **Edit a note's Markdown.** Any note can switch to its Markdown source with `Ctrl+Shift+M` (`⌘+Shift+M`), **Markdown** in the `/` menu or the **Markdown** button in the note's corner, and back to formatted text. Edits are applied block by block as you pause, so mentions and images you did not touch stay as they are and edits from other devices merge; read-only notes show their source read-only.
