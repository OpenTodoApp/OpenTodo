- **External assistants can show your tasks as OpenTodo draws them.** Assistants that support MCP Apps (Claude on the web, desktop and iOS, ChatGPT, VS Code, Goose and Postman) are offered `show_tasks`, `show_calendar` and `show_deadlines`, the blocks of the in-app assistant. They show a list of tasks, a day or week agenda, or the deadlines ahead, inside the answer. A block is a fixed page drawn in the assistant's sandboxed frame. It makes no network request and shows your text as text, and the assistant itself reads only how many items it showed. With an assistant that may make changes, ticking a task in a block completes it like any assistant change. Encrypted accounts get blocks through `ot mcp --decrypt`, which decrypts them on your computer. Assistants without MCP Apps see no change. See [Blocks in the assistant's answer](docs/mcp.md#blocks-in-the-assistants-answer-mcp-apps).
