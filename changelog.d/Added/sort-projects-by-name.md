- **Sort projects by name.** A project's menu offers "Sort sub-projects by name", and the sidebar's Projects menu "Sort by name" for the top level: A to Z with numbers by value, so "0002" comes before "0010" and "2" before "10". It sorts once, with Undo, and the order stays yours to change. Moving a project up or down no longer steps over a project of another workspace, and on the All projects page Alt+Up and Alt+Down move the focused row (#1956).
