- **Open a sub-project inside its parent.** In a project's list, the arrow before a sub-project shows its open tasks and its own sub-projects in place, without leaving the parent; its name still opens it. The tasks complete, edit and move like any row, each with its own project's sharing, and a task dropped on a sub-project or on the Tasks bar moves there with Undo. Each device remembers which sub-projects are open. Thanks to Kechos23 for the idea and the prototype (#1665).
