- **Feature switches per workspace.** Settings → Workspaces → a workspace → **Features** turns Notes, Calendar, Habits, Time tracking, Focus, Routines, Weekly review and Locations on or off for everyone in it; the owner and admins switch, everyone else sees the switches. Off only hides the feature in that workspace (its sidebar entries, commands, keys and task controls), never its data, and the API, CLI, MCP, export and CalDAV are unchanged. Everything starts on, so nothing changes until someone switches. The workspace object gains `features` ([#1637](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/1637)).
