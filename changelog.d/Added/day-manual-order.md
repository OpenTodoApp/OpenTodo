- **Arrange your day by hand in Today and Upcoming.** Both offer **Manual order** and share it, so what you arrange in Upcoming's Thursday is Thursday's order in Today. Drag a task between rows, press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd> or use **Move up** and **Move down** in its menu; it moves within its part of the day, project, priority or label, or its day in Upcoming. Dropped at a line in another day or part, it takes that date or part too, with one Undo. With parts of the day on, **Due date** now lists a day as Time of day does: tasks without a part, then Morning, Afternoon and Evening ([#1480](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/1480)).
