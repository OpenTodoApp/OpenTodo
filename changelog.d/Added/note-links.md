- **Link notes, tasks and projects with `[[`.** Type `[[` in any note (or choose **Link to…** in the `/` menu) to pick a note, task or project, or create a new note on the spot; the link always shows the item's current title, survives renames, and reads as muted text when its target is in the Trash or out of reach. **Linked references** at the end of a note, below a task's notes and as a folded line in a project's note area list what links there, worked out on your device, offline and with end-to-end encryption. Links are plain Markdown (`[Title](?note=<id>)`), so older apps keep them; `?note=` and `?project=` addresses open their item, `ot notes links` lists links on your machine, and the MCP tools `get_note` and `get_task` return `links` and `linked_from` on accounts without end-to-end encryption.
