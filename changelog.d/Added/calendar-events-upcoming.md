- **Calendar events in Upcoming.** Upcoming can show the appointments of each day above that day's tasks, the same read-only agenda as Today: all-day events as chips, then timed events with their time, title, calendar colour and location. **Show calendar events** in Upcoming's menu (next to **Show repeating occurrences**) is off until you turn it on, saved with the account as `user_prefs.upcoming_events`, and separate from Today's choice. Events stay out of the day's count, a filter does not hide them, a day that only has meetings still appears (through the last planned day, up to 92 days ahead), and hiding calendars on this device hides them here too. Offline, the list uses the cached events and says when they were refreshed. The first load grew 0.8 KB ([Calendar events in Today](docs/calendar-events-in-today.md#in-upcoming), #808).
