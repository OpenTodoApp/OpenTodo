- **A native phone app is in testing.** A new OpenTodo app, drawn with the phone's own views, is being built beside the apps in the stores: it signs in through your server's own sign-in page, keeps your tasks in a database on the phone, syncs live and works offline. For now it shows Today and completes tasks, and its first builds go to the project's testers on TestFlight; the apps in the stores are unchanged. [The native app](docs/app.md).
