- **Note templates.** Mark any note **Use as template** in its menu, then start new notes from it with **From template…** next to New note (`Shift+Q`) or **Use template** on the open template. `{{date}}`, `{{weekday}}`, `{{time}}` and `{{title}}` are filled in when the note is made, in your date and time format. Five built-in templates ship: Meeting, 1:1, Trip, Weekly review and Daily, in every language. `template` in the Notes search lists templates only. Routines gain **Create a note from a template**, with the starter *Monday meeting note*.
