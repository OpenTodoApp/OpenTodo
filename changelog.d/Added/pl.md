- **The app is available in Polish (Polski).** Pick it in Settings → Language and region → Language; dates, reminder notifications, the browser extension, the Android and iPhone apps, the Obsidian plugin and the server's email messages follow along. Quick add understands Polish too, in the forms people type: `Dentysta w środę o 15:00`, `Wniosek do piątku` (a deadline), `Raport każdego pierwszego poniedziałku miesiąca`, `codziennie od jutra`, `przypomnij mi 30 minut wcześniej`. See [Quick add syntax](docs/quick-add.md#languages). Translated by @nkkfs, who maintains it (#1413).
