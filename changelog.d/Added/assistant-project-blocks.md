- **The assistant answers questions about projects and progress with the app's own views.** "What's in progress on the website?" shows that column of the project's board, "Is the renovation on track?" its timeline, "What happened on it yesterday?" its latest activity, "How am I doing?" tiles for what you completed, what is overdue, the Inbox and Today against your Today limit, a question about one task shows it in full with its notes, subtasks and comments, and "How is running going?" a habit's month grid and streak. Each is read-only and read from your device. A skill turn adds the board and timeline when you ask from a project, and the statistics and deadlines when you ask from Today.
