- **Subtasks in `ot`.** `ot add --parent` creates a subtask in its parent's project, `ot edit --parent` moves a task under another one or back to the top level (`none`), and `ot list --parent` lists a task's subtasks. `--subtasks` on `ot done`, `ot rm` and `ot edit` applies the change to the whole subtree, as the app does; without it nothing changes from before. `ot list --project` and `--inbox` print subtasks under their parent with its progress, and `ot task show` prints the parent path and the subtasks (`subtasks` in `--json`) (`cli-subtasks`).
