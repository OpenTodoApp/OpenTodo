- **Print a list.** The browser's Print (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P) on the Inbox, Today, Upcoming, a project, a label or a saved view now prints a clean list: the page's title, its groups and every row with its subtasks, dates, project and labels, black on white in any theme, with an empty circle to tick by hand and nothing of the app around it. **Print…** at the end of the list's **…** menu does the same, also in the installed app, which has no browser menu (#778).
