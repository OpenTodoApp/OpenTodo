- **Undo in every list, for single-row moves, and for deleting sections, labels and fields.** Ticking a task in the Filter view, saved views, the dashboard, the reviews, the Daily check and the method lists now offers **Undo**, as the main list does. So do dragging a single row, moving a card to another column, choosing a task's section, moving a row with <kbd>Alt</kbd> and the arrows, deleting a section, deleting or merging a label and deleting a custom field. Completing a task from the main list now also stops its running timer, as it did elsewhere ([#2001](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/2001), [#2002](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/2002), [#2003](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/2003)).
