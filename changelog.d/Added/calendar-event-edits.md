- **Edit your calendar's events in Day and Week.** Turn on **Edit events in OpenTodo** in a connected calendar's **Calendars** dialog to move, resize, rename and delete its events where you see them, with the mouse, the keys or the event's popover, each with Undo. Your calendar stays the truth: an event changed elsewhere in the meantime is left as it is, and repeating events, events with guests and events someone else organised stay read-only. A second switch, **Let assistants edit events**, lets an AI assistant move and rename them over MCP, never delete them, with Undo in the event's popover. Both are off until you turn them on. `ot calendar` lists and changes them too.
