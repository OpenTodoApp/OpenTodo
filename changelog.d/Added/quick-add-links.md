- **A link in quick add becomes an attachment.** Type or paste an `http` or `https` address in quick add, or in a title you are editing, and it is saved as a link attachment of the task instead of staying in the title. A chip shows the site; dismiss it, or put the address in quotes, to keep it as text. `ot add --natural` attaches them too, and `POST /api/v1/quick-add/parse` reports them as `fields.links`. (#1743)
