- **Keep open subtasks.** Turn off **Complete subtasks with parent** in **Settings → General → Tasks** to complete a task while its open subtasks stay open. Those subtasks leave every list, count, search and the badge, fire no reminders and are not listed by AI assistants, the digest or `ot list`, but stay in the completed task's **Subtasks**, where you can still work on them; reopening the task brings them back. Every way of completing a task now follows the choice: AI assistants (MCP and the in-app assistant), automations and routines, Close the day and `ot done`, which now completes open subtasks by default (`--subtasks=false` completes only the task). Open subtasks of tasks completed earlier without them (for example through MCP, `ot done` or the API) are kept the same way: open the completed task to find them ([#1835](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/1835)).
