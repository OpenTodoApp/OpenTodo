- **Keep open subtasks.** Turn off **Complete subtasks with parent** in **Settings → General → Tasks** to complete a task while its open subtasks stay open. Those subtasks leave every list, count, search and the badge, fire no reminders and are not listed by AI assistants, the digest or `ot list`, but stay in the completed task's **Subtasks**, where you can still work on them; reopening the task brings them back. Open subtasks of tasks completed earlier without them (for example through MCP or the API) are kept the same way: open the completed task to find them ([#1835](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/1835)).
