- **Routines show what their AI talks about.** An Ask AI step that answers as text can show tasks, a day or week of your calendar, or your deadlines, as the assistant does in a chat, at most four per run. A morning briefing shows the day's agenda and the tasks to start with under its line in the Inbox card and in the run's detail, live from your own copy and offline. The run keeps only which tasks and days, never text; on an encrypted account the device that runs the routine checks them against its own copy.
