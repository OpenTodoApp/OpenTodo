- **The graph of all notes.** The Notes page has a second mode, **Graph** (`/notes?view=graph`, remembered on the device, and a row under All notes in the sidebar): every note of the workspace and the tasks and projects they link to, as one picture that fills the page. Pan, zoom and fit; point at a note to light its neighbours; click it to open it beside the graph; double-click to centre it with a depth of one to three links; find a note by typing; the search field's chips (`in:`, `label:`…) dim everything outside the topic without moving anything. The layout is the same every time and kept on the device, so a new note appears beside its links while everything else stays put. Drawn on the device from your own copy, offline and with end-to-end encryption; keyboard and screen reader friendly, with the List as its equivalent. Connections gain **Open in graph**. See [See all your notes as a graph](docs/notes-graph.md).
