- **The app is available in Dutch (Nederlands).** Pick it in Settings → Language and region → Language; dates, reminder notifications, the browser extension, the Android app, the Obsidian plugin and the server's email messages follow along. Quick add understands Dutch too: `Tandarts volgende woensdag om 15 uur`, `Bellen morgen om half 4 's middags` (15:30), `Filter vervangen elke 3 dagen na voltooiing`, `herinner me 30 minuten van tevoren`; a two-letter weekday such as `ma` counts only next to a date, so `Bel ma` stays a title. A weekday right before a full date now names that date in every language (`Monday, March 3`). See [Quick add syntax](docs/quick-add.md#languages).
