- Habits for assistants, scripts and routines: the server computes a habit's kept, strength and streaks exactly as the apps do; MCP gains `list_habits`, `get_habit` and `log_habit` (and habit fields in `create_tasks` and `update_tasks`); `GET /api/v1/habits`, `GET /api/v1/habits/{id}` and `POST /api/v1/habits/{id}/log` let a phone shortcut log a day in one request; `ot habits`, `ot habits show` and `ot habits log` do it from the terminal; the assistant gains **Start a habit** and **How are my habits?**, and **Turn this into tasks** reads check-ins ("ran 5k, skipped stretching"); routines gain **Find habits** and **Log a habit**. While Habits is off, the assistant offers none of it. (habits-assistant)
