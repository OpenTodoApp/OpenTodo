- **Workspace activity.** A workspace's Activity page, opened from the workspace menu, lists every change in its projects and Inbox, yours and everyone's, by day, with a date range, several kinds at once, a project and a person; the filters are remembered on the device. Logging or stopping time on a task is now an activity event ("tracked 42m", never the note), Updates and `GET /api/v1/activity` take several kinds (`kind=completed,commented`) and a `time` kind, and `GET /api/v1/workspaces/{id}/activity` serves the page ([Workspace activity](docs/workspace-activity.md)).
