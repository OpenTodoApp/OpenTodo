- Daily notes: one note per day, the same on every device, turned on in **Settings › General › Notes** with a project and a template (the built-in Daily, one of yours, or none). Open today's note with `g j`, from the command palette, the Notes sidebar or Today's header; `[` and `]` move between days, and a day you only look at writes nothing. The routine step **Get the daily note** outputs a day's note for **Add to a note** (daily-notes).
