- **One "when on that day" row in Do on.** The When editor's Do on part asks when on the day with one row, **Any time | Evening | Time** (with **Morning** and **Afternoon** while day parts are on), instead of the This Evening switch and Add time. There a part and a time are one or the other, each choice one change with Undo; <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>E</kbd> and every other way in keep the time as before. The date field shows an example ("tomorrow 3pm, next fri") and takes a date with a time in one go. The quick choices are one compact line, No date is gone (Clear does it), and the time zone reads "Floating" or the city at the end of the time line. The When editor now loads on first use, which takes about 12 KB off the first load. Stored values keep their meaning and 1.0 clients read them as before.
