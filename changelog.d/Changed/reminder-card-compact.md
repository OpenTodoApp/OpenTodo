- **A due reminder takes less room on a phone or tablet.** On a touch screen or in a narrow window the reminder card shows the task and the time on one line each, with **Snooze** (which opens 10 minutes, 1 hour and tomorrow 09:00) and **Dismiss** on one row. Several due reminders show one at a time with a count instead of stacking over the page, and after your answer the keyboard focus goes to the next reminder or back to where you were.
