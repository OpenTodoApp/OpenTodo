- **Many more changes can be undone.** Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Undo in the command palette now take back adding a task with quick add (it moves to the Trash with its reminders), renaming a task, and changing its priority, deadline, estimate, assignee, location, start date, Multi-day, time zone or This Evening. They also take back indenting a task or changing its parent, removing its origin, moving or stretching it in the calendar (with the keys or a drag in the timeline), moving it with the phone's Schedule sheet, parking it in Someday, moving an Inbox task to another workspace, skipping a day of a repeating task or a habit, and a project's rename, colour and icon. Edits of a repeating task are steps too, on the task and the days they carried to, including a changed or stopped repeat rule. An edit made with This and following has no Undo, and parking a repeating task still ends its repetition without one.
