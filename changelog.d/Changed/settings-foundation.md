- **Settings say what blocks you and save one way.** A missing backup, outgoing email that is not configured, two-factor turned off and blocked push notifications show a warning dot on their page and on the section's overview, and a failing webhook shows in red. Switches and choices save as soon as you change them and spring back with the reason if the server refuses. Forms with several fields (attachment limits, focus timer lengths, a workspace's name and colour, outgoing email, single sign-on, speech to text, audit retention) show a save bar with Save and Discard while they have unsaved changes, and leaving the page first asks whether to discard them. Number fields show their unit and allowed range, and choices use one radio style.
