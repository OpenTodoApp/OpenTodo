- **Name your priorities.** Settings → General → Priorities gives the four levels your own words, such as *Must*, *Should*, *Could* and *Won't*. The names show wherever a priority does: the task's facts and priority menus, quick-add chips, priority groups and board columns, the filter builder, bulk actions, the command bar and the assistant's proposals, and a screen reader hears the name with its number ("Must, priority 1"). They sync to your devices, work offline, stay personal in shared projects and are encrypted on accounts with end-to-end encryption. `p1` to `p4` stay the syntax in quick add and filters, and typing `p` after a space lists the levels with their names; the API, the CLI, MCP, CalDAV and emails keep the numbers.
