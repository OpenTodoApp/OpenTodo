- **Reminder notifications say when the task is due.** Under the task's title a reminder now reads **Today 14:00**, **Tomorrow**, a weekday or a date, in your language and date and time formats, in the browser, the Android app and the iPhone app. With **Include task titles** the push also carries the due date, so the notification is complete before the device has synced the task ([#2062](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/2062)).
