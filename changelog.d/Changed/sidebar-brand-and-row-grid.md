- **Rows line up, the sidebar shows your workspace, and Upcoming stays calm.** From 40 rem of row width every list row stands its facts in fixed slots, in the order project, labels, estimate, date, assignee: the date moved from the first slot to the last, so a list's dates form its right edge, an empty slot keeps its width and a slot no row uses takes none. The priority, repeat and count marks are smaller and sit on the title's first line, and a task's part of the day is an icon in the time column instead of "· evening" after the date. The top of the sidebar shows the workspace's own picture (its icon or initial on its colour) and name on one line; the OpenTodo mark and version moved to a quiet line at the sidebar's end that opens About and What's new, with a branded server's logo and name there. The task panel's checkbox sits on the title's first line, its tab row fits the panel (icons with counts when narrow), subtasks are a quiet checklist under their heading, and the estimate editor is one field with ↑ and ↓ and four presets. Upcoming lists its first three days with tasks in full and each later day as one bar with its count and first titles; **Later days: In full** in its display options lists everything (`user_prefs.upcoming_later`, readable by older clients).
