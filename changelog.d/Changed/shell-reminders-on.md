- **Reminders are on in the Android and iOS apps from the start, with the task's name.** They used to be off until you found the switch in Settings → Notifications. The phone asks to allow notifications when your first reminder is there, not at launch. If you never touched the switch, you are asked at your next reminder after updating; if you turned it off, it stays off. Task names can show on a locked phone: turn them off in Settings → Notifications, or hide notification content in the phone's settings. Encrypted accounts keep the generic text.
- **Android asks for exact alarms instead of opening a settings page.** When Android refuses them, the app says once that reminders can be a few minutes late, with Allow; the notice stays in Settings → Notifications until you do.
- **The apps' background refresh ends when it is done.** It ran for a fixed 30 seconds, which cut off a slow sync and wasted battery on a fast one.
- **Settings on iPhone names the iPhone's Settings**, not Android's.
