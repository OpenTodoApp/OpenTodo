- **Personal views hide tasks assigned to someone else.** In a shared workspace, Today, Upcoming, Anytime and Someday now show only tasks assigned to you or to no one, and so do the Today count, the app icon badge, the briefing and the assistant's day summary. The project still lists every task, and Waiting lists them by person. Today's **Hide tasks assigned to others** option is gone, as the rule now always applies ([#1938](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/1938)).
