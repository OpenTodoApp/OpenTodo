- **One click selects a task, a second click edits its title.** In task lists, click a task to select it, then click its selected title to edit it where inline editing is available; Enter opens the task and F2 edits. This replaces the **Click a task to** setting. On a phone, a short left swipe edits the title in the row with Save and Cancel, the full left swipe opens Schedule, and the row menu also offers **Edit title**.
