- **Multi-day tasks stay in their section.** A task that spans several days used to fold into its section's Later as soon as it was created, because its first day is a start date. In a project it now stays among the section's tasks with its range ("24–26 Nov"); it still keeps out of Today and the counts until its first day. A start date on its own still defers the task as before ([#1572](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/1572)).
