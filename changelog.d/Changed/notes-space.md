- **Notes have their own side of the sidebar.** A **Tasks | Notes** control under the workspace name switches the sidebar to your notes: All notes, your pinned notes and the ones you changed last. Opening a note shows it, opening a task shows Tasks again, and `g e` still opens Notes. Notes left the **More** menu; a Notes row you had shown in the sidebar is replaced by the control. The Notes list is one line per note with its project and labels, and the search field takes tokens such as `in:Renovation`, `@errand`, `kind:note`, `linked:Trip`, `orphan`, `pinned` and `changed:<7d`, shown as chips with suggestions; they replace the Source, Project and Label filters, which become chips once.
