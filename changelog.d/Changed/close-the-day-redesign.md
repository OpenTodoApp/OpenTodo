- **Close the day is a calm three-step ritual.** What got done lists what you finished today; What moves puts every unfinished task on one list with Tomorrow, Later or Someday per row (Keep in Today and Done already in its menu, All to tomorrow in one tap, `j`/`k` and the decision keys from the keyboard); Tomorrow's first thing picks the task tomorrow starts with, which becomes tomorrow's focus. Nothing is written until you choose Close the day, on any step, and then one Undo puts the whole evening back, focus included. Esc leaves without changing anything. Someday parks a task; nothing goes to the Trash.
