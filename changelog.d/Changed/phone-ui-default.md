- **A phone now shows the phone screens.** Below 768 px, in the Android and iPhone apps and in a phone's browser, OpenTodo opens in the design made for a phone: its own bar and title on every screen, task lists and the task page written for a thumb. Nothing changes on a tablet or a computer. To go back on a device, turn off **New phone screens** under Settings → Appearance; the choice is kept on that device ([New phone screens](docs/phone-screens.md)).
