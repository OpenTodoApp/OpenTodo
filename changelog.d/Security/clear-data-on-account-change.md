- **A different account signing in clears the previous one's data.** When a session ended without a sign-out (an expired or revoked cookie) and another account signed in on the same device, the new account could see — and, against a fresh server at the same address, upload — the previous account's local tasks. On sign-in, a device whose stored account differs from the one signing in now clears its local copy under the sync lock before anything is shown or synced, and the restore reseed refuses to run while the signed-in account and the local data's account disagree. Signing the same account back in keeps its unsynced work. Signing out or switching accounts also clears what the browser kept for the account outside its local copy: links trusted with "Don't ask again", the Notes search chips with label and project names, comment drafts, import summaries and collapsed rows.
