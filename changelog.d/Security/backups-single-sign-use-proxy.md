- **Backups and single sign-on no longer use a proxy from the environment or follow a redirect to another host.** The backup client took `HTTP_PROXY`/`HTTPS_PROXY` from the server's environment and followed redirects to any host; the single sign-on client also took the proxy. Both now connect directly to the destination or issuer the owner configured, as webhooks and every other outbound request already did. A backup destination may still redirect up to three times within its own scheme and host (a WebDAV folder without its trailing slash, for example); a redirect elsewhere, or from https to http, fails the run, and the sign-on client still follows none. Destinations and issuers on your own network keep working. If your server reached a backup destination or your identity provider only through a proxy, it now needs a direct route. See [Backups](docs/backups.md#destinations) (OpenSpec `operator-clients-no-env-proxy`).
