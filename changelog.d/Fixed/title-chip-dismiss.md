- **The `×` on a chip keeps the words as text again while editing a title (#1463).** In the task panel's title and the inline title editor, pressing a chip's `×` ended the edit first, so the title was saved as parsed: the date left the text and the task got the due date anyway. The press now keeps the caret in the field, the chip is dismissed, and the title is saved as typed with nothing set. Quick add's `×` also leaves the caret in the line, so Enter adds the task right away.
