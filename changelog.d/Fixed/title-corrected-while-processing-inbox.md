- **The title can be corrected while processing the Inbox.** Clicking it showed a cursor, but the decision keys took every letter, so a voice-to-text line could not be fixed without leaving the mode. Click the title or press Tab, type, and Tab, Enter or a click away saves it; Esc puts the old title back. The decision keys work again once the field is left. Enter still opens the task beside the card for comments, subtasks and the rest. ([#858](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/858))
