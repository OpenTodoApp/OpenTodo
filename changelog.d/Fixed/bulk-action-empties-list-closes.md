- **A bulk action that empties the list closes its menu and opens nothing.** On a label page with every task ticked, removing the label through **Actions → Labels…** turned the still-open menu into one task's context menu at the far left of the window, and the last ticked task opened in the side panel. The Actions menu now closes once the ticked tasks have left the list, and the side panel follows the cursor only while its task is still listed (OpenSpec `bulk-menu-after-empty-list`).
