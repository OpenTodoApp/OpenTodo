- **Calendar apps sync large accounts quickly, and subtasks follow their parent between lists.** A calendar app's sync, a single task's fetch or save and the list of calendars read only what they ask for instead of every task of the account: on 10,000 tasks an incremental sync takes about a millisecond instead of a quarter of a second. Moving a task to another list in a calendar app now takes its subtasks with it, as in the app, and a parent named in another list is kept as the app's own data instead of making the task a subtask across projects.
