- **Task titles are readable on a phone after an import.** Tasks imported from Todoist showed the importer's internal id (such as `dfc92cb10aed05a4:1`) at the end of every row, and in the Inbox the line "Imported from todoist" stood beside the title instead of under it, so on a phone the title was cut to a character or two. A row no longer shows an imported task's id: an import without a link shows nothing there, a Trello card shows its icon as the link. On a row narrower than 30 rem any origin is its icon, on a phone it moves to the row's details and the task page, and the Inbox's source line is the line under the title (OpenSpec `row-title-first-on-phones`). ([#844](https://github.com/OpenTodoApp/OpenTodo/issues/844))
