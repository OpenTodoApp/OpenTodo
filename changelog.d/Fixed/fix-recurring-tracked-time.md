- **A repeating task's tracked time starts again with each occurrence.** Completing a repeating task, or skipping a habit, carried its tracked time over to the next date, so the next occurrence started with the old total against its estimate. Rows, the Time tab, the `tracked` filter terms, automation conditions and the assistant's `tracked_minutes` now count only the current occurrence's entries; the Time tab adds **All occurrences** with every entry's sum, and statistics and the CSV export are unchanged. Nothing is rewritten: past entries keep their place.
