- **"Today and repeat every other Saturday" keeps the Saturday.** Quick add stored "every other Saturday" as `every 2 weeks` from the task's date, so a line with its own date ("today") repeated on that weekday instead, and "and repeat" or "starting" stayed in the title (#1016). Rules can now say `every 2 weeks on saturday`: the date you type is the first occurrence and completing it moves to the next right Saturday. "and repeat", "repeating", "and then" and their equivalents leave the title with the rule, and "starting tomorrow", "starting 16 Nov" or "from Monday" next to a rule set where the series starts, in all six quick-add languages. The importer reads "every other Monday", and CalDAV serves and reads the rule as `FREQ=WEEKLY;INTERVAL=2;BYDAY=SA`.
