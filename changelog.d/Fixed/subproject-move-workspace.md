- **Moving a sub-project to another workspace works, and Undo of a move puts the project back.** "Move to workspace…" on a sub-project said it had moved, but the sub-project stayed where it was, because it follows its parent's workspace. It now leaves its parent and becomes a top-level project in the other workspace, with its own sub-projects and tasks. Undo of any project's move also put the project in the Trash instead of moving it back (#1660).
