- **Sign in with Google (and other providers that refuse apps) works in the Android and iPhone apps.** The provider's page now opens in the system's sign-in sheet (a Custom Tab on Android, Safari's sheet on iPhone) and, after you confirm with Continue on a page naming your account, the app comes back signed in, instead of Google's "disallowed_useragent" error or a sign-in that ended in the browser. Linking the provider from Settings in an app works the same way. Nothing to configure; behind a reverse proxy, let `/api/v1/health` through (#1553). Passkeys at a self-hosted provider such as Rauthy work this way on Android too (#1539).
