# Design

## Context

See proposal.md for motivation. What exists today and shapes the approach:

- **One "today" hook, many consumers.** `useToday()` in `web/src/app/useViews.ts` returns `todayISO()` and re-reads it every minute and on a zone change (web-app "Date-dependent views follow the clock"). Eleven screens read it: `useListModel` (Today, Upcoming, Anytime, Completed, habit chips), `Sidebar` (counts, pinned views, the review markers), `CommandPalette`, `FilterView`, `CalendarView`, `MonthView`, `PlanPanel`, `StatsView`, `DailyCheckToast`, `useMethodInput` (Loop home, Daily check, Next, Waiting) and `useViewData` (saved views). The pure libraries under them (`lib/views.ts`, `lib/filter/evaluate.ts`, `lib/stats.ts`, `lib/habits.ts`, `lib/review.ts`, `lib/method/*`) already take `today` as a parameter. Changing what the hook returns moves most of the app at once.
- **"Day of an instant" is computed in five places** as `todayISO(new Date(ms))`: `lib/stats.ts` (`deviceDay`, the default of the injectable `input.dayOf`), `lib/method/pulse.ts` (`dayOf`), `lib/logbook.ts` (the Completed groups), `lib/dayLists.ts` (`completedOn`, the Display mode) and `lib/review.ts` (`dailyDue`, `reviewAgo`). These must agree with the hook, or a task completed at 01:30 would be "today" in the list and "yesterday" in the count.
- **`todayISO(date)` is also a plain calendar formatter.** `addDaysISO`, `RemindersField` ("in 10 minutes" → the date of that instant), `listActions.addDays`, `DateTimeSettings`, `Clock` and `templates.ts` use it to turn a `Date` into `YYYY-MM-DD`. Those must *not* shift: a reminder at 01:40 must store the calendar date 01:40 falls on, or it would fire in the past. So `todayISO` keeps its meaning and the logical day is a second function.
- **Writes that take `today`.** `completeTask`, `skipOccurrence`, `detachOccurrence`, `setRecurrence` (`lib/ops.ts`), `planComplete`, `dateOfPick`, `planDue` (`lib/bulk.ts`), quick add (`lib/nlp/parse.ts` reference date), triage's `t` ("due today"), `VimLayer` and `TaskRow` ("Starts" vs "Started") default to `todayISO()`. For a recurring series the occurrence record's id and date come from the series' due date (`occurrenceDate(series, today)` falls back to `today` only for a series without a due date), and the next due of a completion-relative rule counts from `today` (`followingOccurrence(rule, due, today, completedOn = today)`).
- **The server** knows `today` in three places: `internal/httpapi/recurring.go` `localToday` (the server's clock, for REST and CalDAV completions of recurring tasks), `internal/agenttools` (the caller's time zone, passed by the tool), and `internal/digest` (the user's digest time zone, `Input.Today` and `Input.dayOf`). The digest already reads `week_start` from the user's `user_prefs` row (`service.go` `Build`), and its numbers are pinned to the device's by `internal/digest/testdata/stats_fixture.json`, evaluated by both `stats_test.go` and `web/src/lib/stats.test.ts`. Push reminders fire at `fire_at` instants and never ask for a day.
- **Preferences plumbing.** The synced `user_prefs` row "me" (date-time-preferences D1) is read by `readUserPrefs` (`lib/userPrefs.ts`), published by `DateTimePrefsProvider` as React context plus a module snapshot (`setDateTime`), and edited in `app/DateTimeSettings.tsx` with three `Select`s and a preview. The schema in `internal/sync/ops.go` lists the fields as `kString` without restricting values; clients read unknown values as the default.
- **Interface budget** (docs/interface-guide.md): the Settings panel is a form, not a list; no shortcut is added; no sidebar destination changes.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- One function decides the logical day; every consumer of "today" and of "the day of an instant" goes through it, so the list, the count, the streak and the digest can never disagree.
- The default (`00:00`) is bit-for-bit today's behaviour: every existing test passes unchanged.
- Purely a reading rule: no stored value, op or record changes meaning; devices converge exactly as before.

**Non-Goals:**
- Changing `todayISO`'s calendar meaning or the floating `due` model.
- Half-hour or arbitrary minutes in the Settings control (readers accept them; the control offers whole hours).
- The CLI, MCP and assistant tools' "today" (they take a time zone from the caller; see Open Questions).
- Per-view, per-project or evening-shifted boundaries.

## Decisions

**D1. Storage: `user_prefs.day_start`, a `kString` holding `HH:MM`.** Added to `Schema` in `internal/sync/ops.go` beside `week_start`, to `UserPrefs` in `web/src/lib/types.ts` (`day_start: string`) and to `USER_PREFS_DEFAULTS` in `web/src/lib/ops.ts` as `''` (unset reads as `00:00`). The server does not restrict the value, like the other preference strings; `readDayStart(row)` in `lib/userPrefs.ts` accepts `^([0-5]\d|06):[0-5]\d$`-shaped strings with `06:00` as the upper bound and returns minutes after midnight (0 to 360), anything else 0. The field is not content (no `e2ee.ContentFields` entry), derives no activity event (the `user_prefs` fixture op already proves the entity is ignored), and is exported and restored with the row by the existing export and importer paths without a change. Alternatives: a number of minutes (`kNumber`); rejected because the spec names a time and a string reads the same in the export and in `docs/sync.md`'s table. A per-device `meta` entry; rejected for the same reason the three formats are synced: the boundary is a property of the person.

**D2. Two functions in `lib/dates.ts`, one meaning each.** `todayISO(now)` stays the calendar date of an instant. New: `logicalDay(ms: number, dayStartMinutes: number): string` returns `todayISO(new Date(ms - dayStartMinutes * 60_000))` — subtracting the offset from the instant before taking the local date handles DST days and month and year ends through `Date` itself, and is a no-op at `0`. `logicalToday(dayStartMinutes = currentDayStart(), now = Date.now())` is the convenience for "now". The current day start is a module snapshot set by the provider (`setDayStart(minutes)`, read by `currentDayStart()`), the same pattern as `setDateTime`/`currentDateTime`, so pure helpers that cannot take a hook (`lib/ops.ts`, `lib/bulk.ts`, `lib/logbook.ts`) read it as a default argument and tests pass it explicitly. Alternative: make `todayISO()` itself logical; rejected because of the reminder and calendar-formatting uses listed in Context, which must keep calendar dates.

**D3. `useToday()` returns the logical today and ticks at the boundary.** The hook reads the day start from the provider's context (`useDateTimeFormat().dayStart`), keeps the one-minute interval, and lists `dayStart` with `zone` in its effect dependencies so a synced preference change re-reads at once. Nothing else in the eleven screens changes; the pure libraries receive the logical day through their `today` parameter as they do now. The provider's memo key gains `|${day_start}`.

**D4. Day of an instant: inject the snapshot.** `lib/stats.ts` `deviceDay` becomes `(ms) => logicalDay(ms, currentDayStart())` (still overridable through `input.dayOf`, which the shared fixture uses); `lib/method/pulse.ts` `dayOf`, `lib/logbook.ts`, `lib/dayLists.ts` `completedOn` and `lib/review.ts` `dailyDue`/`reviewAgo` do the same. The Loop pulse therefore agrees with Statistics without touching `loop-home-refresh` (its spec says "local days"; after this change "local day" means the logical day everywhere, which is the definition the statistics capability now carries). `web/public/push-sw.js` does not compute days.

**D5. Writes take the logical today too.** The `today` defaults in `completeTask`, `skipOccurrence`, `detachOccurrence`, `setRecurrence`, `planComplete`, `dateOfPick`/`planDue`, quick add's reference date, triage's `t`, `VimLayer`, `TaskRow`'s "Starts/Started" and `Properties`' deadline countdown become `logicalToday()`. Reasoning per case: a recurring occurrence record keeps its date from the series' due date, so completing yesterday's habit at 01:30 records yesterday whatever `today` is; a completion-relative rule ("every 3 days after completion") counts from the day the person is living in, which is what they mean by "in 3 days"; "due today" from triage or quick add means the current logical day; "tomorrow" typed at 01:30 means the next logical day, which is the calendar date the sun will rise on. These writes store ordinary calendar dates computed on the writing device; nothing about them is reinterpreted later, so convergence is untouched. Alternative: keep writes on the calendar date; rejected because the Daily check would then write `focus_date` for a day the screen does not consider today, and the focus would vanish at midnight, the very bug this change removes.

**D6. The server: one feature follows, two stay, and why.**
- *Digest* (`internal/digest`): `Build` already fetches the `user_prefs` row for `week_start`; it also reads `day_start` through a `DayStart(string) int` helper (minutes, 0 on anything invalid) and sets `Input.DayStart`. `Input.dayOf(ms)` becomes `time.UnixMilli(ms).In(loc).Add(-dayStart).Format(day)` and `Input.Today` is computed the same way from `now`. The shared fixture gains `"dayStart": "04:00"` and two cases around a week boundary; both test suites evaluate it (`stats_test.go` passes `DayStart`, `stats.test.ts` passes `dayOf = (ms) => logicalDay(ms, minutes)` in the zone the fixture names, composing `dayInZone`). The digest is sent at the user's chosen hour in their time zone; a user whose day starts at 04:00 and digest at 07:00 gets a digest for the week that ended three hours earlier, which is the expected reading.
- *REST and CalDAV completions of recurring tasks* (`internal/httpapi/recurring.go` `localToday`): unchanged. The record's date is the series' due date; `today` only decides catch-up for a calendar rule and the start of a completion-relative rule, and the server has only its own clock, not the user's wall time. The CLI and scripts complete tasks in daytime; the window in which the two differ (00:00–06:00, server clock equal to the user's) is accepted and documented in `docs/api.md`'s note on recurring completions.
- *Push reminders* (`internal/push`): fire at `fire_at` instants and do not compute days. Unaffected.
- *Habit records*: an occurrence record (`series_id`, `completed_at`, `skipped`) has no day field; its day is the occurrence's due date, derived from the id. Unaffected, by construction.
- *MCP and assistant tools* (`internal/agenttools`): `today` is the caller's calendar date in the time zone the tool was given. Unchanged in this change (see Open Questions).

**D7. Settings control and preview.** `DateTimeSettings.tsx` adds a fourth `SettingRow` after the week start, a `Select` with the seven whole hours `00:00`…`06:00` formatted with `formatTime` (so `12h` users see `12:00 AM`, `1:00 AM`, …; `00:00` reads "Midnight (default)"), a `description` line "Work done before this time counts for the previous day", and in the preview a line "Today: {weekday date}" from `logicalToday()`, re-rendered by the minute tick. The value writes through `setUserPref('day_start', 'HH:MM')`. A keyboard user tabs to the select and uses the arrows, like the other three. The help id stays `settings/date-time`.

**D8. Nothing else reads the clock for "today".** A guard test (like `dateFormatGuard.test.ts`) fails on a new `todayISO()` call with no argument outside `lib/dates.ts` and an allow-list (`DateTimeSettings` sample dates, `RemindersField`, `listActions.addDays`, `templates.ts`, `Clock`/`DisplayScreen`, which pass an instant or want a calendar date). It keeps the next screen from reintroducing the midnight day.

## Data model

Additive: `user_prefs.day_start` (`kString`, `"HH:MM"`, unset means `00:00`). No field renamed, retyped or removed; no Dexie version bump (the `userPrefs` table is keyed by `id` only and the field is not indexed). `docs/sync.md`'s entity table gains one row. Reads: the row, `task.completed_at`, `created_at`, `due`, `start`, `deadline`, `review.daily_at`, `focus_date`. Writes: only the preference itself, and the same ops the screens already write.

## Size and resources

No dependency. Web bundle about +0.6 KB gzipped (two small functions, the select and preview, the catalog strings in six languages). The Go binary grows by under 1 KB (one schema entry, the `DayStart` helper). Container image and runtime memory unchanged; the digest does one more map lookup per build. On the client the one-minute tick already exists; the logical-day function is a subtraction before a date format.

## Interface budget

- Rows of chrome above a list's first item: unchanged (Settings is a form; no list screen changes its chrome).
- Task properties visible by default: unchanged.
- Primary sidebar destinations: unchanged.
- Vertical scroll regions: unchanged.
- Shortcuts: none added; `web/src/lib/shortcuts.ts` is not touched.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [A reader that keeps using `todayISO()` for "today" disagrees with the rest after 00:00] → the guard test of D8 and the migration list in tasks.md; the default `00:00` makes any miss invisible until a user sets a day start, so the scenario tests run with `04:00`.
- [DST: on the spring-forward night the local hour 02:00 does not exist] → `logicalDay` subtracts from the instant, not from wall-clock fields, so the date is whatever `Date` reports for the shifted instant; the boundary moves with the clock like midnight does today. Covered by a test at the 2026-03-29 and 2026-10-25 transitions in a European zone.
- [The digest's weekday hour is before the day start] → a digest at 02:00 with a day start of 04:00 covers "last week" as the user sees it at that moment (the week before the one still being lived); the Settings card already states the hour, and `docs/notifications.md` (the digest section) gets one sentence.
- [A second device without the synced preference shows a different today for a while] → nothing is written because of it; after sync both devices read the same row (spec "Two devices, different clocks").
- [Filter periods `this week`/`last week` and the Week view's "today" column] → both read `ctx.today`/`useToday()` and move together with Statistics, which the statistics spec requires ("Week boundaries SHALL be the same as those of the filter periods").
- [Translations: "Midnight (default)" and the explanation line in six languages] → `make i18n-check` fails on a missing entry; the task adds the six catalog entries.

## Migration Plan

Additive schema entry and a reading rule; ships with the web bundle and the binary in one release. An older client ignores the field (unknown fields are kept as stored and not shown) and keeps the midnight day; an older server rejects an op with an unknown field as it does today (date-time-preferences "Older server" behaviour), the device keeps its local value and nothing else is affected. Rollback is reverting the commit; a stored `day_start` is then simply unread.

## Open Questions

- Whether `ot today`, the MCP `today_summary`/`list_tasks` tools and the assistant should apply `day_start` on top of the time zone they are given. The server already holds the row, so it is a small follow-up (`internal/agenttools/data.go` `render` and the CLI's local date); it does not change these specs or the approach here.

## Implementation notes

- **Wall clock, not subtraction (D2, D6).** `logicalDay(ms, dayStart)` (web) and `digest.LogicalDay(t, loc, dayStart)` (server) compare the instant's local wall time with the day start and take the previous calendar date when it is earlier, instead of subtracting the offset from the instant. Subtracting a fixed duration would keep the previous day for an extra hour after the day start on the spring-forward night (04:00 CEST minus four hours is 23:00 CET). The spec's wording ("the previous date while the local time is before the day start") is what both functions implement; the tests pin 2026-03-29 and 2026-10-25 in `Europe/Zurich`.
- **Zone composition in tests.** `dayInZone(zone, dayStart = 0)` in `lib/stats.ts` gained the optional day start and applies the same wall-clock rule in the given zone; `stats.test.ts` and `dates.test.ts` use it where the task said "`logicalDay` composed with `dayInZone`" (the device's `logicalDay` reads the device zone, so it cannot be composed with another zone directly).
- **Fixture shape (4.2).** `dayStart` is a per-case field rather than a top-level one, so the four existing cases keep midnight and the two new cases ("last week / this week, Monday start, day start 04:00") apply `04:00`. The two new tasks (`n1` at 01:30 and `n2` at 04:00 on Monday 12 October in Zurich) also fall in the existing "this week" cases, whose expected `completed` and `perDay` were raised by two; Go and TypeScript agree on every case.
- **Readers moved beyond the list.** Also on the logical today: the default `today` of `describeDue`, `describeDeadline`, `formatDateHeading` and `formatDate` in `lib/dates.ts`; the day ranges of `created`/`completed <op> date` filter terms (`lib/filter/evaluate.ts` `startOfDay` begins a day at the day start); `RecurrenceField`'s preview, `HabitHistory`'s default, `FirstRun`'s sample project, the template start day and `template.day` (`TemplateDialogs.tsx`, `lib/templates.ts`), `SavedViewView`'s placement, and `CalendarView`'s now line (minutes are counted from the logical today's midnight, so between midnight and the day start the line is not drawn on the previous day's column).
- **Reminders keep the calendar.** The quick-add reminder phrases pair the calendar date with the clock time of the same instant (`todayISO(now)`, `nowHHMM(now)`), and `RemindersField`'s default "tomorrow 09:00" stays the next calendar day, as the docs say ("reminders still happen at their real moment").
- **Guard allow-list (D8).** `web/src/lib/todayGuard.test.ts` also refuses `todayISO(new Date())` and `todayISO` passed as a function, ignores comments and imports, and its allow-list holds only `lib/dates.ts` and `app/RemindersField.tsx`: the other files the design named (`DateTimeSettings.tsx`, `listActions.ts`, `templates.ts`, `display/Clock.tsx`, `display/DisplayScreen.tsx`) no longer call `todayISO()` without an instant, so allowing them would only weaken the guard.
- **Settings value with minutes.** A stored day start that is not a whole hour (`03:30` from a newer client) is shown as an extra option with its own value, so the select never misstates it; the seven whole hours are offered as designed. The row is also findable in the Settings search (`settingsIndex.ts`).
- **Not changed:** the activity feed's day headings (`lib/feed.ts`, a log of instants) and the server's own "today" (REST, CalDAV, MCP, CLI), as D6 and Open Questions say; `docs/api.md` states it.
- **Keyboard check (3.1).** happy-dom does not move a native select with the arrow keys, so `dateTimeSettings.test.tsx` checks the select is labelled and in the tab order and that the change events the arrows fire write the value; the rest of the arrow behaviour is the browser's own.
- **Smoke (6.1).** Run as a script instead of by hand: the release binary on a fresh data directory, Chromium with its clock installed at 01:30 on Thursday 15 October 2026 (`Europe/Zurich`). Result: at the midnight default Today shows Wednesday's task under Overdue; after choosing 04:00 in Settings the preview names Wednesday 14 October, Today lists Wednesday's task with no Overdue group and leaves Thursday's out, Upcoming starts with Tomorrow, a task completed at 01:30 is under Today in Completed, Statistics opens without errors, a second profile signed into the same account shows `04:00` and the same preview after sync, and when the clock runs past 04:00 Thursday's task appears in Today without a reload. No page errors. The habit month grid, the Loop home's focus and pulse and the sidebar markers were not driven in the browser; their day-start behaviour is covered by `habits.test.ts`, `pulse.test.ts`, `review.test.ts` and `useToday.test.tsx`.
- **Visual baselines.** The Storybook story `settings-app--date-and-time` now shows the day start row and the "Today" preview line, so its baselines (`web/src/ui/__screenshots__/settings-app--date-and-time--{light,dark}-*.png`) are stale. They were not regenerated: `make ui-baselines` needs a Docker daemon, which this session does not have; they come from CI's ui-check-report artifact. No docs screenshot shows the Date and time panel.
