# Proposal

## Why

With every task that has no project already scheduled, the Inbox shows its Inbox zero state: the large illustration and the sentence, and below them the folded group "Planned, no project" with its count. The owner, looking at it: "'inbox zero' big image and below 6 tasks. feels weird." The celebration and a list of six tasks compete on one screen; the illustration says "nothing here" while the page still shows work.

## What Changes

- While the Inbox's "Planned, no project" group is shown, the Inbox zero state is one quiet line: the same sentence, "Inbox zero: every task has a place.", in the compact empty-state form, without the illustration.
- With nothing below it, the Inbox keeps the illustration as today. Process the Inbox's own Inbox zero screen is unchanged.

Out of scope: the group itself, its folding, the sentence and the other empty views.

Presentation only: no data, no synced field, no migration, no environment variable. Not breaking.
