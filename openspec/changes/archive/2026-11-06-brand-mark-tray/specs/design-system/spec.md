## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Brand mark
OpenTodo SHALL have one brand mark and one wordmark, kept as a single SVG source in the shared UI folder, from which every other brand asset is generated. The mark SHALL be an open tray whose two upper ends turn inward, with a tick dropping into its opening. It is drawn as two strokes, the tray and the tick, on a 24-unit grid with a 2-unit stroke and round caps and joins. The tick SHALL stay within the tray's width, and the lips SHALL stay clear of the tick by at least the visible gap between the tick and the tray's sides. The mark is never a tick inside a closed square and never a generic checkbox. It SHALL exist in two variants:
- **mono**, for use in the interface: the tray in `currentColor` and the tick in the accent colour.
- **colour**, for icons on other people's surfaces (home screens, browser toolbars, store listings, launchers): the mark on a filled rounded square in the brand's dark slate, the tray in white and the tick in the accent.

The wordmark SHALL be the name "OpenTodo" set in the bundled interface typeface and SHALL never appear inside the mark. The mark SHALL stay legible at 16 px and SHALL be rendered by the app at 16, 20, 24 and 32 px only from the SVG. Platform files SHALL exist only where a platform requires them, and SHALL be generated by a script, never edited by hand:
- the web app manifest icons and the Apple touch icon;
- the extension icons;
- the iOS app icon, an opaque 1024 px image that fills its square;
- the Android launcher icon and notification icon, as vector drawables. The notification icon is the mono mark as one white silhouette on transparency.

The mono mark SHALL meet 3:1 against every surface it is placed on in both themes and the high-contrast override. The colour mark's tray and tick SHALL each meet 3:1 against its own square. The source SVG SHALL be under 2 KB and carry the project's licence note.

#### Scenario: One source, every asset
- **WHEN** the brand script runs after the source SVG changes
- **THEN** the favicon, the 192 and 512 px manifest icons, the maskable icon, the Apple touch icon, the three extension icons, the iOS app icon and the two Android icons are regenerated from it, a test compares each file against the source and fails when one is stale, and no other brand file exists in the repository

#### Scenario: Legible at 16 px
- **WHEN** the workbench renders the mono mark at 16 px in light and dark
- **THEN** the tray and the tick are both distinguishable in the baseline image, the lips do not touch the tick, and the accessibility gate reports no contrast failure for the mark against the sidebar, canvas and surface tokens

#### Scenario: Mono follows the text
- **WHEN** the mark is placed in a row whose text tone changes on hover
- **THEN** the tray's stroke changes with the text and only the tick stays in the accent

#### Scenario: Colour variant on a home screen
- **WHEN** the app is installed on a phone with a maskable icon
- **THEN** the icon shows the mark on the slate square with the whole tray and tick inside the safe zone, and no part of either stroke is cut by a circular or squircle mask

#### Scenario: iOS app icon
- **WHEN** the iOS shell is built after the brand script ran
- **THEN** its app icon is the colour mark filling the square with no transparent pixel, as the App Store requires

#### Scenario: Android notification icon
- **WHEN** the Android shell posts a reminder notification
- **THEN** the status bar shows the mark's tray and tick as one white silhouette, and the launcher shows the colour mark

#### Scenario: Not a checkbox
- **WHEN** the mark is reviewed in the workbench's Brand story beside the row checkbox at the same size
- **THEN** the two are different shapes: the checkbox is a closed ring or a square, the mark is an open tray with a tick dropping into its opening

### Requirement: Illustrations
The client SHALL draw illustrations for empty and finished states in one calm style: inline SVG line drawings built only from the design tokens, with round line caps and joins and no gradients, shadows, text, raster images or external files.
- **Stroke weights:** two, a main line for the object and a fine detail line, with strokes in the muted text colour.
- **Fills:** the surface as the object fill and the sunken surface for ground shapes. At most one accent shape, filled with the theme's accent at low opacity and outlined in the accent text colour.
- **Motif:** every illustration SHALL sit inside an open loop, the illustrations' own motif, drawn in the fine line with its opening at the upper right, and its accent shape SHALL sit in or leave through that opening. The loop is not the brand mark and does not change when the mark changes.

Each illustration SHALL render correctly in light and dark themes and every colour theme by following the tokens, SHALL be decorative (hidden from assistive technology, the message is in the text beside it), SHALL fit a 160 by 120 px box, SHALL weigh at most 4 KB of markup, SHALL not animate when the user prefers reduced motion, and SHALL be shown as a story in the workbench in both themes. The empty-state pattern SHALL accept an illustration in place of its icon. The first illustration SHALL be Inbox zero: an empty tray with a paper plane leaving through the loop's opening.

#### Scenario: Follows the theme
- **WHEN** the user switches from the light theme to the dark theme and to another colour theme while the Inbox zero state is shown
- **THEN** the illustration's strokes and fill change with the tokens, and no colour stays from the previous theme

#### Scenario: Not read by a screen reader
- **WHEN** a screen reader reaches the Inbox zero state
- **THEN** it reads the heading "Inbox zero: every task has a place." and the action, and nothing for the illustration

#### Scenario: Style gate
- **WHEN** a contributor adds an illustration that uses a hard-coded hex colour or a raster image
- **THEN** the UI lint fails and names the file

#### Scenario: Weights and fills gate
- **WHEN** a contributor draws a line with a third stroke width, fills a shape with the canvas colour, or fills two shapes with the accent
- **THEN** the UI lint fails naming the file and the line

#### Scenario: Objects stand out in the dark theme
- **WHEN** an empty state is shown in the dark theme
- **THEN** the drawing's object is lighter than the canvas behind it, and the accent shape's outline is the bright accent

#### Scenario: Unchanged by the brand mark
- **WHEN** the brand mark changes to the tray mark
- **THEN** every illustration keeps its open loop and renders byte-identical markup to before
