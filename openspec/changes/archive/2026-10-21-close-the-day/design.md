# Design

## Context

See proposal.md for motivation. What exists today:

- The synced personal `review` row (`internal/sync/ops.go` `Schema["review"]`, `web/src/lib/types.ts` `Review`, defaults in `web/src/lib/ops.ts` `REVIEW_DEFAULTS`) holds `completed_at`, `daily_at`, `monthly_at`, `focus_date`, `focus`. It resolves to the author's personal scope, syncs to the user's devices only, converges per field by LWW, is exported, derives no activity event and is never encrypted. `web/src/lib/review.ts` holds the pure readers (`dailyDue`, `storedFocus`, `focusTasks`, `reviewAgo`) and the one-op writers (`finishReview`, `finishDailyCheck`, `finishMonthlyLook`, `toggleFocus`), all through `mutate('review', 'me', …)`.
- The weekly review screen (`web/src/app/review/ReviewView.tsx`, `review-guided-stepper`) is a stepper: `ReviewProgress` (circles in phase groups, fits 375 px), one `Card` with an eyebrow "Step n of N · phase", an `h2` title that takes focus, a badge, content, and Back / Next below; `[` and `]` are `review.prev-step` / `review.next-step` (scope `view`, views `['review']`). `ReviewProgress` and the step card are currently bound to the weekly review's `STEP_IDS`, `PHASE_OF`, `PHASE_TITLES` and `STEP_TITLES` from `lib/review.ts`.
- The Daily check (`DailyCheck.tsx`) is one page: Inbox count, Overdue, Today, `f` toggles the focus; `useListKeys` gives it `j`/`k`/`c`/Enter on its rows. Routes under `/review/<step>` are dispatched in `Shell.tsx` (`daily`, `monthly`, else `ReviewView`), all in the lazy `ReviewBundle` chunk.
- Bulk actions (`web/src/lib/bulk.ts`) are pure planners (`planDue`, `dateOfPick`, `mergeChanges`, `toProposed`) applied with `applyBatch(changes, { source: 'bulk', label })` from `web/src/lib/undo.ts`, which writes ONE `mutateMany`, stores the inverse and the clocks, and is undone by `undoBatch` skipping fields changed since. The Shell renders the toast from `web/src/app/bulk/toastStore.ts` (`showBulkToast(label, undoId)`), and `Ctrl/⌘+Z` undoes while it shows. `parkUndoable` in `lib/undoActions.ts` defines what "Someday" writes: `{ start: 'someday', due: null, evening: false }`.
- Today is `todayList(live, today)` in `web/src/lib/dayLists.ts` (`inToday`: open, and a hard date reached or started), grouped by `TaskListView` / `GroupedList`; the view's last element is the grouped list (`LogbookMore` and `AddSection` only on other views). Statistics (`web/src/lib/stats.ts`) counts completions per local day with `compute(input, period)` over `countedTasks` (live tasks, the user's share of shared projects, no skipped habit occurrences).
- End-to-end encryption: `web/src/lib/e2eeSync.ts` encrypts an op when `isContentField(entity, field)` and the entity is personal; its scope resolver returns `true` for every entity it does not know (`review` included), so a `review` content field needs only the list entry. The server mirrors the list in `internal/e2ee` `ContentFields` (checked in step by `internal/sync/schema_fixture_test.go` and `web/src/lib/schemaFixture.test.ts` through `internal/sync/testdata/schema_fixture.json`), and `PurgeSupersededContent` takes `e2ee.AllContentFields()`.
- The Loop home (`web/src/app/method/LoopHome.tsx`, in-flight change `loop-home-refresh`, tasks complete) shows the Review move as three rhythm lines (`Rhythm`) and chooses the next move with `nextMove`. The sidebar's Review entry (`Sidebar.tsx` `reviewMarker`) shows a Due badge or `reviewAgo`.
- Shortcuts live in `web/src/lib/shortcuts.ts`; `shortcuts.test.ts` fails on a conflict within a scope or with an ancestor scope. In the `review` view (`VIEW_SCOPES.review = []`) the taken `view` keys are `[`, `]`, `r`, `q`, Enter, `a`, `j`, Space, `k`, `f`; the list keys (`useListKeys`) take `c`, `s`, `n`, `e`, `d`, `b`, `x`, `1`–`4`, `Shift+…`, Delete; the vim layer takes `o`, `i`, `x`, `:`, `d d`. `g Shift+D` is free (`g d` is the Day calendar, `Shift+D` alone duplicates in a list; a `g` sequence never meets either).
- No time-tracking capability exists: ROADMAP.md sets "a focus timer with time tracking" aside, and no change of that name is in `openspec/changes/`.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- One short ritual, three steps, built from the stepper that exists; the review screens keep one look.
- Every number on the first step is a pure function of the rows, reusing the Statistics definitions, so every device shows the same figures and the Loop pulse, Statistics and Close the day never disagree.
- The carry-over is one batch through the bulk path: one `mutateMany`, one toast, one Undo.
- Two additive fields, no server logic.

**Non-Goals:**
- No new history (closed days, streaks): the row keeps only the latest stamp and note.
- No change to the Daily check's steps, the weekly review's steps or the Monthly look.
- No server-side reading of the note (digest, MCP, agent tools, CLI): it is content and the server treats it as opaque.
- No notification, toast or marker that says the day is "due" to be closed.

## Decisions

**D1. One screen at `/review/close`, in the review chunk.** `web/src/app/review/CloseDay.tsx`, exported from `ReviewBundle.tsx`, dispatched in `Shell.tsx` when `route.step === 'close'` (beside `daily` and `monthly`; the router already carries `{ view: 'review', step }`). Alternative: a dialog over Today. Rejected: three steps with a list and a text field need a page, and a route gives the palette, the shortcut and the Loop home one target.

**D2. The stepper becomes reusable, in place.** `ReviewProgress` gets optional props `ids`, `phaseOf`, `phaseTitles`, `stepTitles` (defaulting to the weekly review's constants, so `ReviewView` does not change), and the step card header plus the Back / Next bar move from `ReviewView.tsx` into `web/src/app/review/StepCard.tsx` (`position`, `title`, `icon`, `badge`, `onBack`, `next: { label, onClick, primary }`, children). Both screens render the same markup; the review's tests (`review.test.tsx`, `#review-step-title`, `data-review-step`, `data-review-nav`) keep passing because the ids and data attributes stay on the card. Close the day has one phase ("Close the day") and three steps `summary`, `carry`, `note`; `STEP_IDS`-style constants live in `lib/review.ts` as `CLOSE_STEP_IDS`, `CLOSE_STEP_TITLES`. `[` and `]` reuse `review.prev-step` / `review.next-step` (same view `review`, so no registry change for them). Alternative: a second copy of the card. Rejected: two copies drift, and the file limit (1,100 lines) makes an extraction in `ReviewView.tsx` welcome anyway.

**D3. The summary is pure and reuses Statistics.** `daySummary(input: MethodInput, review, now, weekStart): { planned, done, focusDone, focusTotal, unfinished: Task[] }` in `lib/review.ts`:
- `unfinished` = `todayList(live, today)` with live = not deleted and not in an archived project (as `DailyCheck` filters), minus tasks the user may not edit (`canEditTasks(roleOf(project, memberships))` from `lib/sharing.ts`, the rule `MethodRules.working` uses; they are shown with Keep only).
- `done` = `compute(statsInput, { start: today, end: now })`.perDay[today] (the same `countedTasks` and local-day rule as the per-day chart, so "Agrees with Statistics" holds by construction).
- `planned` = `unfinished.length + done`: what Today still lists plus what left it by completion today. A task completed today that was never in Today (due next week, done early) counts as planned and done; accepted, named in Risks, far simpler than reconstructing Today at an earlier moment, which the rows cannot do.
- focus: `storedFocus(review, today)` resolved against live rows; `focusTotal` = stored ids still live (not deleted), `focusDone` = of those, completed. Shown only when `focusTotal > 0`.
Tracked time: not computed. The step has a `<dl>` of figures; a time-tracking change would add one `<div>` to it and nothing else here, which is why the proposal calls the line optional.

**D4. Carry-over decisions are device state until Apply, then one batch.** The step holds `Map<taskId, Decision>` with `Decision = { kind: 'tomorrow' } | { kind: 'day'; date: string } | { kind: 'someday' } | { kind: 'keep' }`, default `tomorrow` for every editable task, in React state (nothing stored, nothing synced; leaving the screen drops it). Each row is the ordinary `TaskRow` (project chip, `showProject`) followed by a `role="radiogroup"` of four `Button`s (`aria-checked`, arrow keys within the group), inline from 640 px and wrapped under the row below. Keys on the selected row (`useListKeys` keeps `j`/`k`/`c`/Enter, as in the Daily check): `close.tomorrow` `t`, `close.pick` `p`, `close.someday` `y` (the same letter triage uses for Someday), `close.keep` `h`, all `section: 'review'`, `scope: 'view'`, `views: ['review']`, listed in the cheat sheet under Reviews. None of `t`, `p`, `y`, `h` is bound in the `view` scope for `review`, in the `list` scope or in the vim layer, so the conflict test passes without `overrides`. `p` opens the existing `DateField` (`web/src/app/datetime/DateField.tsx`) in a `Dialog`, as the bulk Actions menu's "Pick a date…" does.
`planCarryOver(decisions, tasks, today): Change[]` in `lib/review.ts` builds the changes with the bulk planners: `planDue([task], 'tomorrow' | iso, today)` for Tomorrow and a day (keeps a time of day, un-parks, as bulk-actions "Reschedule" promises), `{ start: 'someday', due: null, evening: false }` for Someday (the patch `parkUndoable` writes), nothing for Keep; `mergeChanges` then `applyBatch(await toProposed(changes), { source: 'bulk', label })` with the label `bulkLabel({ kind: 'due', pick: 'tomorrow' }, n)` ("3 tasks rescheduled") when every decision is a date, else a new string "3 tasks carried over". `showBulkToast(label, entry.id)` shows the one toast; `Ctrl/⌘+Z` and the toast's Undo call `undoBatch`, which restores every task in one step and skips fields changed since (bulk-actions "Undo a bulk action in one step", unchanged). Blocked tasks (the Today "Blocked" group) are listed like the others and default to Tomorrow: their date is kept honest and the lock chip still shows. Recurring tasks: a due change is the ordinary reschedule of the current occurrence, the same as the bulk Date menu. Alternative: write each decision as its own op when chosen. Rejected: the spec wants one undo and one toast, and a decision changed twice before Apply would write twice.

**D5. Two additive fields on `review`, note as content.** `closed_at` (`kNumber`, Unix ms; 0 = never) and `tomorrow_note` (`kString`, one line, trimmed, at most 500 characters enforced by the field's `maxLength`; the server applies only its general `MaxValueBytes` as for every string field). "Day closed" is derived as the Daily check's due rule is: `dayClosed(closedAt, today) = closedAt > 0 && todayISO(new Date(closedAt)) === today`; no `closed_date` field is needed, and the same rule on every device gives the same answer for the same local date. `yesterdaysNote(review, today) = review.tomorrow_note.trim() || null` when `closed_at > 0` and its local date is before `today`, else null; so the note shows the next morning (and later, until the day is closed again), and the first close of a day hides it without a write. Writer: `closeDay(note, now)` = `mutate('review', 'me', { closed_at: now, tomorrow_note: note })`, one op pair, offline through the outbox. Convergence: per field LWW, so two offline closes may keep one device's time and the other's note (spec scenario "Two closes offline"); accepted, each is the latest write of its own field. The note is user-written text, so `CONTENT_FIELDS.review = ['tomorrow_note']` in `web/src/lib/e2ee.ts` and `ContentFields["review"] = {"tomorrow_note"}` in `internal/e2ee`; the e2ee scope resolver already treats `review` as personal, encryption and the purge route follow from the lists, and the shared schema fixture pins both sides. The weekly-review spec's sentence "never encrypted" is narrowed to the times and focus. Alternative: store the note on the Daily check's `focus`-like fields or as an Inbox task. Rejected: a note is not a task (it would need processing), and a separate field keeps the Daily check's writes untouched.

**D6. Where it is offered.** (a) `TaskListView.tsx` renders, for `route.view === 'today'` only and below the grouped list, one quiet row `data-close-day`: a `Button variant="ghost" icon="moon"` "Close the day", or the text "Day closed, 18:42" (from `useReviewRow()` and `dayClosed`) with the same button as "Close again". It is content below the list, not chrome, and is read-only safe (the Today view is always the user's own). (b) `LoopHome.tsx`'s Review move gets a fourth line after the three rhythms: "Day: closed 18:42" or a link "Close the day", a plain `<a>` to `/review/close`; `nextMove` is unchanged (`loop-home-refresh` D3), so the Review move is never lit for this. (c) The palette: `{ label: msg('Close the day'), route: { view: 'review', step: 'close' }, icon: 'moon', shortcut: keysOf('go.close'), keywords: 'end of day evening tomorrow carry over' }` in `palette/registry.ts` `ROUTES`. (d) `go.close` `g Shift+D` in `shortcuts.ts`, handled in `Shell.tsx` beside `go.daily`. The `moon` icon exists in the vendored set; if `make ui-lint` says otherwise, `sunset` or `clipboard-check` is used and no icon is added.

**D7. Markers.** `Sidebar.tsx` `reviewMarker`: when `dayClosed(review?.closed_at, today)` and nothing is due, the ago text becomes `t('Day closed')` with the time in the `sr-only` text; when something is due the Due badge stays and the sr text appends "; the day is closed". `DailyCheck.tsx` shows `yesterdaysNote` as the first element under the header: a `Notice tone="info"` with the eyebrow "Yesterday's line" and the note as text, `data-daily-note`, read-only (editing is the next close). With encryption the note is decrypted on the device like a title; `UNDECRYPTABLE` shows the placeholder.

**D8. Finishing.** The last step's primary action calls `closeDay(note)` then `navigate({ view: 'today' })`, where the end-of-list row now reads "Day closed". Closing twice is allowed and simply writes a newer time (and note). Carry-over is applied on step 2 by its own Apply button, not by the final action, so Undo of the batch and the close stamp stay independent (spec "Undo does not reopen the day"); Next on step 2 with unapplied decisions asks nothing and applies nothing, but the step shows "Not applied" beside the count so nothing is silent.

**D9. Stories and tests.** `review.stories.tsx` gains "Close the day" and "Close the day · note" under `Screens/Review and statistics` with `withSeed`/`withClock` (light/dark × 375/1280 baselines, axe). Tests: `web/src/lib/review.test.ts` for `daySummary`, `planCarryOver`, `dayClosed`, `yesterdaysNote` (fixed `today` and rows, no DOM); `web/src/app/review/closeDay.test.tsx` renders the Shell at `/review/close` with seeded tasks (`// @vitest-environment happy-dom`) for the spec scenarios; `rhythms.test.tsx` for the sidebar text; `loopHome.test.tsx` for the Review move's line; the Go side needs only the fixture update and `internal/e2ee` `fields_test.go` extended with `review.tomorrow_note`.

## Data model

Additive only, on the existing `review` entity (id `me`, personal scope):

| Field | Type | Meaning | Content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `closed_at` | number (Unix ms), default 0 | the latest Close the day | no (plaintext) |
| `tomorrow_note` | string, default `''` | the line for tomorrow written at that close | yes (encrypted for encrypted accounts) |

No field is renamed or removed. `Schema["review"]` in `internal/sync/ops.go`, `Review` in `web/src/lib/types.ts`, `REVIEW_DEFAULTS` in `web/src/lib/ops.ts`, `internal/sync/testdata/schema_fixture.json`, `internal/e2ee` `ContentFields` and `web/src/lib/e2ee.ts` `CONTENT_FIELDS` change together (AGENTS.md rule 3). An older server rejects the two fields as unknown and the device keeps them locally (the existing "Older server" behaviour of the entity). The export already serialises every `review` field. No migration: Dexie's `review` store is keyed by id and untyped per field. Task writes are ordinary `due`, `start`, `evening` ops.

## Size and resources

No dependency. Web: about +3 KB gzipped in the review chunk (`CloseDay.tsx`, `StepCard.tsx`, the planners) and under 0.5 KB in the main bundle (the Today row, four shortcut rows, one palette command, the sidebar branch). Binary: two schema entries and one content-field entry, a few hundred bytes. Container image and runtime memory unchanged; the summary runs `compute()` once over the live tasks for a one-day period, memoised on its inputs, less work than the Loop pulse already does.

## Interface budget

- Rows of chrome above the first item: on Close the day, the page header and the progress indicator (2 of 2), as on the weekly review; the step card's header is content. On Today nothing is added above the list; the close row is below it.
- Task properties visible by default: rows are `TaskRow` with the project chip; the decision group is a control beside the row, not a property, and shows no further task data.
- Primary sidebar destinations: unchanged (no destination; the marker reuses the Review entry).
- Vertical scroll regions: the content column only (1 of 3); the date picker is a dialog.
- Shortcuts: `go.close` (`g Shift+D`, global), `close.tomorrow` (`t`), `close.pick` (`p`), `close.someday` (`y`), `close.keep` (`h`) (view `review`), all registered in `web/src/lib/shortcuts.ts` and passing `shortcuts.test.ts` without `overrides`; `[`/`]` are the existing review entries.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [`planned` counts a task completed early that Today never listed] → the first step's hint says "what Today listed plus what you finished today"; exact reconstruction would need a snapshot of Today, which is new history and out of scope.
- [Applying the carry-over and closing are two actions; a user may close without applying] → step 2 shows "Not applied" beside its count until Apply, and the last step's intro repeats how many tasks are still in Today. One combined action was rejected because Undo would then have to choose between tasks and stamp.
- [A note typed on two devices offline ends with one device's time and the other's note] → per-field LWW is the entity's rule everywhere; the spec names the outcome; a note is one line and the next close overwrites it.
- [`g Shift+D` next to `g d` (Day calendar) is easy to mistype] → the cheat sheet and the palette show both; Shift distinguishes the pair exactly as `g i` / `g Shift+I` already do.
- [The `review` entity was documented as "never encrypted"; adding one content field changes a sentence people may rely on] → the spec narrows the sentence, `docs/e2ee.md` lists the field, and the shared fixture test fails if one side forgets.
- [Decision keys `t p y h` in the `review` view while the Daily check and the walk share that view id] → the handlers are registered only while Close the day is mounted (`useShortcutScope` in `CloseDay.tsx`), as `review.focus` is only in `DailyCheck.tsx`; the registry allows one declaration per key in the view.
- [Long translations of the four decisions on a 360 px phone] → the group wraps to two rows of two below 640 px; the 375 px story covers German and French through the Storybook language switch.
- [This change builds on the in-flight `loop-home-refresh` wording of the Loop home] → archive `loop-home-refresh` first; the `open-loops` delta here restates that version with one added line and two scenarios.

## Migration Plan

Ships with the web bundle and the server binary together; both sides are additive. A newer client against an older server keeps its stamp and note locally and the carry-over ops sync (the entity's existing "Older server" behaviour). Rollback is reverting the commit: the two fields stay in the op log as unknown fields of a known entity, which the server already tolerates. Docs screenshots: a new `close-day` shot in `scripts/docs-screenshots.mjs` waiting for `#review-step-title`.

## Implementation notes

- **`y` and time tracking.** Time tracking (since this design was written) binds `y` to `list.track` in the `list` scope. The registry conflict test does not see it because `VIEW_SCOPES.review` is `[]`, but the review screens mount `useListKeys`, and the `list` scope is consulted before `view`. `CloseDay.tsx` therefore passes `extra: { 'list.track': () => false }` to `useListKeys`, so `y` on a Carry over row falls through to `close.someday` and never starts a timer (asserted in `closeDay.test.tsx`). `t`, `p`, `h` and `g Shift+D` are free as planned; `shortcuts.test.ts` passes without `overrides`.
- **"Today" is the logical today.** `dayClosed` and `yesterdaysNote` use `logicalDay(closedAt, currentDayStart())` (day-boundary), not `todayISO(new Date(closedAt))`; a close at 01:30 before a 04:00 day start belongs to the evening before.
- **Started tasks leave Today.** `planDue` keeps a past start date, and a started task stays in Today whatever its due date, so the spec's "all three leave Today" would fail for the started task. `planCarryOver` also writes `start: null` for a task whose start date has come when it is carried to Tomorrow or a day. A task in Today only because its deadline has come stays in Today after Tomorrow: Close the day never moves a deadline.
- **Read-only tasks.** `daySummary` keeps them in `unfinished` with `editable: false` (the spec lists them with Keep only and counts them as planned) instead of removing them as D3 says; `planCarryOver` writes nothing for them.
- **`done`** is `compute(...)` over the one-day period `{ start: today, end: today }`; the test checks it against this week's `perDay` for today.
- **Loop home line.** The rail tile is one link, and a link cannot sit inside a link, so the "Close the day" / "Day: closed <time>" line is rendered below the tile's link (`data-loop-extra`), still inside the Review tile.
- **Sidebar text.** "Day closed, <time>" replaces the "Last review" description and tooltip; with a due rhythm, "; the day is closed" is appended to the due marker's text.
- **Palette ids.** Route commands with a step now get the id `go:<view>:<step>` (`go:review:close`), so they do not collide with `go:review`.
- **Stories.** `CloseDay` takes an optional `initialStep` so the stories can open on Carry over and on the note step.
- **Tests' location.** The sidebar marker scenarios are in `review/review.test.tsx` (where the inline-sidebar harness lives) and the Daily check note scenarios in `review/closeDay.test.tsx`, not in `rhythms.test.tsx`.
- **Tracked time.** The spec ("How the day went") says no time is shown, so time tracking's totals are not added; a later change could add one figure to the `<dl>`.
- **Screenshots and baselines.** `close-day-{light,dark}.webp` were generated with `node scripts/docs-screenshots.mjs close-day` and are shown in docs/method/rhythms.md. `loop-*.webp` (the Review move's new line) and `today-*.webp` (the row at the end of Today, below the fold) are now stale; regenerating them put the demo's live reminder toast over the shot, so they were left as they were. Visual baselines for the two new stories need `make ui-baselines` (Docker), not available here.
