# Proposal: brand-kit

## Why

The owner wants the logo files people actually use (for slides, a README, an avatar, a press mention or the GitHub social preview) in the main repository. Until now they existed only as a kit on one machine. The repository held just the files platforms require, and the design-system spec forbade any other brand file. Kept in the repository and generated by the brand script, the kit is easy to find, carries the licence, and can't drift from the mark.

## What Changes

- A top-level `brand/` folder with a README:
  - `svg/`: the mark on light and on dark, black, white, the colour icon (rounded and square), and the lockup (mark + "OpenTodo") on light and on dark;
  - `png/`: the colour icon at 16, 32, 48, 64, 128, 180, 192, 256, 512 and 1024 px; the mark variants at 256, 512 and 1024 px; the lockups at two sizes; a 512 px avatar; the GitHub social preview (1280 × 640) and a default social card (1200 × 630).
- A second brand source, `web/src/ui/icons/wordmark.svg`: "OpenTodo" in Inter SemiBold as outlined paths (Inter is under the SIL Open Font License, which allows this), so the lockups open correctly without the font.
- `scripts/brand-icons.mjs` writes the whole kit. The stamp and the test cover it, plus a check that `brand/` holds only generated files.
- The design-system "Brand mark" requirement names the kit and the wordmark source, replacing "no other brand file exists" for these files.
- The app's own wordmark (`BrandWordmark`) stays live text, as its spec requires.

Not **BREAKING**.

## Out of scope

- Shipping `brand/` in the site bundle (the bundle already has its own `brand/` with the mark and wordmark). A later change can switch the bundle to this folder.
- Uploading the social preview and the avatar to GitHub, Discord or the stores. That is done by hand in their settings.
- A press page on the website.

ROADMAP: no new item; this rounds off the tray mark (`brand-mark-tray`).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `design-system`: "Brand mark" gains the wordmark source and the brand kit.

## Impact

- **New:** `brand/` (about 30 PNGs and 9 SVGs, about 450 KB) and `web/src/ui/icons/wordmark.svg` (about 2.5 KB).
- **Changed:** `scripts/brand-icons.mjs` and its test, `web/src/ui/icons/brand-icons.json`, `docs/design-system.md`.
- No change to the app's bundle, binary or image: `brand/` isn't built or embedded.
