## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Running entry pill in the footer
While the user has a running time entry on a screen 768 px or wider, the sidebar footer SHALL stay one row: a pill in the accent-soft tint SHALL take the place of the account name beside the avatar, holding a timer icon, the elapsed time in tabular figures (`h:mm` from one hour, ticking every minute), the task's title truncated, and a Stop control inside the pill. Activating the pill SHALL open the task, or, when the entry is the running work stretch of the user's Focus session, the Focus screen with that session; activating Stop SHALL end the entry without opening the task. The avatar SHALL stay and the account menu SHALL still open from it with the person's name as the menu's name. On the icon rail the pill SHALL show the elapsed time alone, with the title and Stop in its tooltip and menu, and the menu SHALL offer "Go to Focus" in place of "Open the tracked task" for a Focus session's entry. When no entry runs the account name SHALL be shown as before. The pill SHALL be announced once when tracking starts and once when it stops, never per tick. The elapsed time is computed from the local entry, so it is right offline and follows a stop made on another device after sync.

#### Scenario: Pill replaces the name
- **WHEN** an entry on "Write the report" has run for 1 hour and 5 minutes on a 1280 px screen
- **THEN** the footer is one row reading avatar, "1:05 · Write the report" in a pill with a Stop control, then the assistant, bell and sync dot, and no second line exists above or below it

#### Scenario: Stop from the pill
- **WHEN** the user activates Stop inside the pill
- **THEN** the entry ends with the local time, the pill is replaced by the account name without the row moving, "Tracking stopped" is announced once, and the task panel does not open

#### Scenario: Pill of a Focus session
- **WHEN** a Focus session runs and the user, on Upcoming, activates the pill's time and title
- **THEN** the Focus screen opens with the running session, and the task panel does not open

#### Scenario: Account menu while tracking
- **WHEN** the user activates the avatar while the pill is shown
- **THEN** the account menu opens with the person's name as its name and Settings, Help, Something isn't working, Keyboard shortcuts and Sign out

#### Scenario: Stopped elsewhere
- **WHEN** the entry is stopped on another device and this device syncs
- **THEN** the pill disappears and the account name returns without a reload
