Guides

Short, task-based guides for everyday use.

  • Tasks and projects

    Add and edit tasks, write notes, break work into subtasks, set priorities and organise everything into projects and sub-projects.

  • Views

    Inbox, Today, Upcoming, Assigned to me, Completed and the calendar, plus views you save yourself, dashboards and the view each device opens on.

  • Sections and boards

    Split a project into stages with sections, and switch it to a board to move cards between columns by drag or keyboard.

  • Labels and filters

    Tag tasks across projects with labels, then find exactly what you need with the filter language and save it as a view.

  • Recurring tasks and reminders

    Make a task repeat every week, month or weekday, skip an occurrence, and get reminded at the right moment.

  • Time blocking

    Estimate how long tasks take, then place them on the Day or Week calendar to see whether your plan fits the day.

  • Sharing and comments

    Share a project with people on your server, choose what they can do, assign tasks, and talk it through in comments with @mentions.

  • Notifications

    The bell for assignments and mentions, reminder pop-ups inside the app, and push notifications on each device when the app is closed.

  • Offline and sync

    How OpenTodo keeps working without a connection, what the sync status means, and what happens when two devices change the same task.

  • Keyboard shortcuts

    Every shortcut in OpenTodo, from quick add to the calendar, plus the optional Vim keybindings.

  • Account security

    Sign in with a passkey, add two-factor authentication, change your password and check where your account is signed in.

  • Import and export

    Download everything you have as one file, and bring tasks in from a spreadsheet, another OpenTodo server or other task apps.

  • Trash and history

    Restore deleted tasks and projects from the Trash, see every change made to a task, and bring back an earlier value.

  • Settings

    A tour of Settings, what each section does, which choices belong to one device and which follow your account.

  • Troubleshooting and FAQ

    Fixes for the usual hiccups, from a task that seems to be missing to reminders that don't arrive, and answers to common questions.
