@AGENTS.md

OpenSpec workflow for Claude Code: `/opsx:propose "<idea>"` plans a change, `/opsx:apply` implements it, `/opsx:archive` merges its specs into `openspec/specs/`. Project constraints for artifacts live in `openspec/config.yaml`.

## The bar

Make it as good as Linear's website or Apple Notes: simple purpose, simple architecture, simple design, simple implementation. Cut taglines, helper text and secondary labels; if a screen needs them to make sense, fix the layout instead. Every decision should improve the user experience (UX), the developer experience (DX) and the agent experience (AX) at once, without breaking anything.
