# The Quarter calendar and Plan spans

The Calendar's range switch is **Day | Week | Month | Quarter | Plan | Year**. Quarter sits between Month and Plan: the horizon between a month's task rows and the Year's twelve compact grids. Switching to or from it keeps the date you are looking at, and the sidebar remembers the last mode on this device.

## See the quarter

**Quarter** (choose it in the range switch, or press <kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>q</kbd>) shows the three calendar months of the quarter holding that date: Q1 is January–March, Q2 April–June, Q3 July–September and Q4 October–December. The title is a translated `Q{n} {year}` (`T4` in French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese). It reads local rows only and works offline.

- Three compact month grids sit side by side on a wide screen, with the same density marks, over-capacity dots and event rings as [the Year](months-and-year.md#see-the-year): none for an empty day, then a short, a longer and a full bar for 1–2, 3–5 and 6 or more things planned.
- It is read-only. Choose a day to open it in Day, or a month's name to open it in Month. On a phone each month is one touch target opening Month.
- <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd> step one quarter keeping the day, and <kbd>t</kbd> comes back to today. The keyboard grid is the Year's: arrows for a day or a week, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> / <kbd>Page Down</kbd> for a month, <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> for the month's edges, <kbd>Enter</kbd> for the day and <kbd>Esc</kbd> for the page header.
- The calendar's filter, the [other calendars](other-calendars.md) toggle and **Show repeating occurrences** apply as in Year. In a project's Calendar layout only that project's tasks count.

## Choose a Plan span

**Plan** shows consecutive days as columns. Its page menu offers **3 days**, **7 days**, **14 days** (the default) and **21 days**, plus **Custom…** for any whole number of days from 2 to 90. A custom value is refused with a message until it is a whole number in that range; **Save** applies it, and the checked **Custom…** item then shows the current length. **Cancel** leaves the span alone.

The span lives in this device's grid preferences, like the day length and the grid hours; it is never synced, so another device signed into the same account keeps its own span. The page title, the columns, the projected occurrences and the week-based navigation all follow the chosen length. See [Plan the next days](calendar-views.md#plan-the-next-two-weeks) for what a column shows.
