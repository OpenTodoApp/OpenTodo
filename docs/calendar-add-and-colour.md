# Add and colour on the calendar

<picture><source srcset="images/week-dark.webp" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)"><img src="images/week-light.webp" alt="The Week calendar with timed tasks as coloured blocks and an Unscheduled lane under each day."></picture>

Two ways to plan faster in [Day, Week, Month and Plan](calendar-views.md): add a task right where it belongs, and colour tasks by what matters to you.

## Add a task at a time

Double-click an empty quarter hour of Day or Week to add a task due then: type its name (and anything [quick add](quick-add.md) understands) and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. It appears as a block at once, even offline. A double-click in a day's Unscheduled lane adds one with the date only, and in the Month a double-click on an empty part of a day adds one due that day. Under a mouse, the quarter hour (or the Month day) under the pointer is lightly highlighted.

Right-click an empty place instead, or press and hold it on a touch screen, for a small menu: **Add task here…**, and in Day and Week **New frame here…** (a [frame](frames.md) on that weekday, from that time) and **New slot here…** (a 30-minute [slot](slots.md)). Holding a task block keeps its usual meaning, and a double-click on a task still opens it.

From the keyboard, <kbd>q</kbd> adds a task on the focused day: in Day and Week the selected task's day, else today. A time you type, like `at 4pm`, sets the time on that day.

## Colour by project, label or priority

Blocks and rows take their project's colour. To see something else at a glance, open the calendar's **More actions** menu and choose **Colour by**: **Project**, **Label** or **Priority**. The choice applies to Day, Week, Month and Plan, and this device remembers it.

- **Label**: a task takes the colour of its first label in your label order (the order of the Labels page) that isn't the default grey. Tasks without a coloured label are grey.
- **Priority**: priority 1, 2 and 3 take the red, orange and blue of the priority rings; priority 4 is grey.

Calendar events and frames keep their own look.

**Boards** have the same choice, shown as a coloured edge on the left of each card: in the project menu (**Colour by…**) of a project's [board](sections-and-boards.md), or in **Display options** of a [board view](filters.md#saving-views). It is one choice for every board on this device; **Project**, the default, leaves the cards as they are. Neither choice is synced.
