# Set up in ten minutes

## 1. Choose how you work

In **Settings → You → How you work** (or in the start guide on your first visit):

- **Simple lists**: just your tasks and lists, nothing else.
- **Lists with method hints**: your sidebar stays as it is, and each screen gets a short hint on its purpose in the method, with a link to this guide.
- **Full Open Loops method**: the **Loop home**, a page that shows what needs your attention today, opens first; the sidebar shows Loop home, Inbox, Today, Next, Waiting and Review; and the app offers a Daily check each day.

The start guide says what Open Loops is before you choose, and **What is Open Loops?** opens this guide's overview. The choice syncs to all your devices and changes no task. You can switch any time. Choosing the full method also offers to open on the Loop home on this device.

### Energy levels

Pick your next task by how much energy you have. Turn on **Energy levels** in the same section (off by default, whatever you chose above) and give a task **Deep**, **Medium** or **Low** energy: from **Add** on the task, the row's menu, or `~deep`, `~medium` or `~low` in [quick add](../quick-add.md). A row shows the word when set. The filter `energy: low & unblocked & today` is an end-of-the-day list of easy wins; save it as a [view](../filters.md). Turning the switch off hides the choice, not the values: a task that has an energy still shows it, with **Clear**.

## 2. Create your contexts

A context is where or how you can do a task. When you choose method hints or the full method and have no context yet, the app offers four context labels: `@home`, `@computer`, `@calls` and `@errands`. Accept, or make your own: any label starting with `@` is a context.

## 3. Empty your head

Press <kbd>q</kbd> and catch everything on your mind, one line each, for five minutes. Do not sort anything yet.

## 4. Clarify

Press <kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>p</kbd> and decide each item: do it, schedule it, move it to a project, delegate it, park it in Someday or trash it. See [Clarify](clarify.md).

## 5. Walk your projects

Open the weekly review (<kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>r</kbd>), go to **Projects**, and give each project a next step with <kbd>q</kbd>. From now on, work from **Next** and come back to the review once a week.
