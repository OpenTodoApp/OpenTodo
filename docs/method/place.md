# Place

Give every item one home. When each task lives in exactly one place, you know where to look, and anything not in front of you can safely be ignored.

## The homes

| If the item | Place it | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| belongs to an outcome with several steps | a project | <kbd>m</kbd> in triage, or the project chip |
| must happen on a day | Scheduled | give it a due date (<kbd>s</kbd> in triage) |
| cannot start before a day | Later | a start date (<kbd>Shift+N</kbd> in a list) |
| waits for someone or another task | Waiting | assign it (<kbd>a</kbd>) or set **Blocked by** (<kbd>b</kbd>) |
| is a maybe | Someday | <kbd>y</kbd> in triage, <kbd>n</kbd> in a list |

The full list, with the screen for each, is in [Places](places.md).

## Waiting

**Waiting** (<kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>Shift+W</kbd>) lists everything assigned to someone else, grouped by person, and everything blocked by another open task. Look at it in the weekly review and follow up where something has stalled.

## Hide what is not now

Someday and deferred tasks stay out of Today, Upcoming and Next until they matter. You lose nothing: Someday has its own view and the review shows it once a week.
