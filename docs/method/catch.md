# Catch

Get whatever is on your mind into the Inbox the moment it appears. Do not decide anything yet: catching is fast precisely because it asks for nothing but the words.

## Ways to catch

- Press <kbd>q</kbd> anywhere and type. Dates and labels typed in the title are understood ([Quick add](../quick-add.md)), but you do not need them.
- Forward an email to your personal address, share from another app, use the browser extension, or speak ([Capture from anywhere](../capture.md)).
- In a terminal, `ot add "Call the plumber"` ([Command line](../cli.md)).

Everything lands in the **Inbox**, on every device, offline too.

## Catch everything

The method only works if the Inbox holds everything. Small things count: "buy light bulbs" nags as much as "plan the move". When you notice something you have not caught, catch it now.

Once a week the review's **Mind sweep** asks a few questions ("Calls to return", "Promises to someone") to find what slipped through. See [Review](review.md).
