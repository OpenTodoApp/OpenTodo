# Review

Look over the whole system on a rhythm, so you can trust it. Trust is what lets you stop holding things in your head: you know that everything is caught, placed and will come back when it matters.

## Three rhythms

- **Daily check** (5 minutes, <kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>Shift+R</kbd>): the Inbox, what is overdue, today's focus.
- **Weekly review** (30 minutes, <kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>r</kbd>): the whole system, in three phases.
- **Monthly look** (20 minutes): your areas, old Someday items and the four-week trend.

Details are in [Rhythms](rhythms.md) and in [Weekly review and statistics](../review-and-statistics.md).

## The weekly review

1. **Get clear**: empty the Inbox, then a **Mind sweep** to catch what is still on your mind.
2. **Get current**: what you completed and what is overdue, what is **Waiting**, every project one at a time, and the week ahead.
3. **Get creative**: skim **Someday** and promote what you now want to do.

The project walk is the heart of it: one card per project, with its outcome, its next action and whether it has stalled. Press <kbd>r</kbd> when it looks right, <kbd>q</kbd> to add a next step.

## Keep the rhythm

The sidebar's Review entry shows **Due** when a review is late. In Open Loops first, the app offers the Daily check once a day. A skipped review is not a failure: the next one simply takes a little longer.
