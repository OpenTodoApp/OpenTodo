# Do

Work from your next actions, by context, one step at a time. Choosing what to do is easy when every option is small, concrete and possible right now.

## The Next view

<picture><source srcset="../images/next-dark.webp" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)"><img src="../images/next-light.webp" alt="The Next view: the next action of four projects under Anywhere, each with its date and project."></picture>

**Next** (<kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>Shift+N</kbd>) shows the next action of every project: its first task, in the project's order, that you can do now. A task is left out while it waits for another open task, is assigned to someone else, is deferred to a later start date or parked in Someday. Complete one with <kbd>c</kbd> and the project's following step takes its place.

## Contexts

A **context** is any label whose name starts with `@`: `@home`, `@computer`, `@calls`, `@errands`. Next groups its tasks by context, so at the computer you look at `@computer` and in town at `@errands`. Tasks without one are under **Anywhere**. Add a context in triage with <kbd>l</kbd>, or in a list with <kbd>Shift+L</kbd>.

## Today and focus

Due dates still matter: **Today** shows what must happen today. The [Daily check](rhythms.md#daily-check) lets you pick up to three tasks as today's focus, shown on the Loop home.

## One next step

A project without a next action is stuck. The weekly review's project walk finds them and asks for the next step. See [Review](review.md).
