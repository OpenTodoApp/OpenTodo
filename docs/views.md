# Views

<picture><source srcset="images/sidebar-dark.webp" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)"><img src="images/sidebar-light.webp" alt="The sidebar with the built-in views at the top, counts next to Inbox, Today, Assigned to me and Trash, then the Views, Projects and Labels groups."></picture>

Views are built from the tasks on your device, so they work offline and update the moment something changes. OpenTodo comes with a handful, and you can save your own.

## The built-in views

The built-in views are at the top of the sidebar, the less frequent ones under **More**. Press <kbd>g</kbd>, then a letter, to open one: <kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>i</kbd> for the Inbox. A project can be a list, a board or a timeline. [The built-in views](built-in-views.md) lists them all.

## Process the Inbox

**Process** in the Inbox header (<kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>p</kbd>) shows one task at a time with a key for each decision, each with Undo. See [Process the Inbox](process-the-inbox.md).

## Plan your day with Today

Tasks from earlier days first under **Earlier**, then today's by time, with **This Evening** and **Blocked** below. Anything you add here is due today. See [Today and Upcoming](today-and-upcoming.md#plan-your-day-with-today).

## Plan your week with Upcoming

Upcoming groups everything due after today by day, from **Tomorrow** on, and lists a task with a deadline on its deadline day. See [Today and Upcoming](today-and-upcoming.md#plan-your-week-with-upcoming).

## Review your week

**Review** (<kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>r</kbd>) walks a [weekly review](review-and-statistics.md) in seven steps with live data, and **Statistics** (<kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>Shift+S</kbd>) shows what you [completed, created and let slip](statistics.md), computed on your device.

## Anytime, for when you have time

Anytime lists the open tasks with no due date, no deadline and no start date, grouped by project. See [Anytime](built-in-views.md#anytime-for-when-you-have-time).

## Park ideas in Someday

Someday holds the tasks you parked with <kbd>n</kbd>. They stay out of Today, Upcoming, Anytime and the counts. See [Someday](built-in-views.md#park-ideas-in-someday).

## Narrow a list by duration

The **Filter** action in a list's header shows only quick wins, tasks under 30 minutes, tasks over an hour or tasks without an estimate. See [Narrow a list by duration](built-in-views.md#narrow-a-list-by-duration).

## Save your own views

Describe what you want in the **Filter** view, press <kbd>s</kbd> and name it. The saved view is pinned to the sidebar with a live count and syncs to all your devices. See [Saved views](saved-views.md#save-your-own-views).

## Build a dashboard

A dashboard puts up to six saved views side by side, for a morning review on one screen. See [Build a dashboard](saved-views.md#build-a-dashboard).

## Display mode

**Display mode** shows a view full screen in large type with a clock, for a wall tablet or an e-ink panel. See [Display mode](display-mode.md).

## Choose where the app opens

Each device can open on a different view, a display included: pick it in **Settings → General → Start view**, or with **Start view on this device** in a saved view's menu. If that view is deleted, the device opens on the Inbox.

## Tips

- Views that sort by date (Today, Upcoming, labels, filters) can't be reordered by hand. Projects and the Inbox can.
- [Filter queries](filters.md) has ten ready-made examples to copy.
- Track habits? Save the query `habit` as a view named "Habits": every habit with its streak, one click from its month history.
- A project with [custom fields](custom-fields.md)? Save `cost > 1000` or `no client` as a view, or turn on **Columns** in its list.
