# Notes

<picture><source srcset="images/notes-dark.webp" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)"><img src="images/notes-light.webp" alt="The Notes page: a search field, the Source, Project and Label filters, and five notes of tasks with their first line, project, labels and when they changed."></picture>

The **Notes** page lists everything you have written: the notes of your tasks, the notes of your projects, and notes that are not tasks at all, such as meeting notes, a reference page for a project or a packing list you keep for next year. Open it from **More** in the sidebar, or press `g` then `e`.

## The list

Notes you pin come first, under **Pinned**; every other note follows under **Recent**, with the most recently changed first, 50 at a time; **Show more** adds the next 50, and the count beside it says how many there are in all. A note counts as changed when its text changes: moving a task to another day does not move its note up the list.

Each row shows where the note lives (a task, a project or a standalone note), its title, the first line of its text, the project, the labels and when it last changed. A completed task and an archived project are marked. Open a row to read or edit it: a task's note opens the task, a project's note opens the project with its note ready to edit, and a standalone note opens beside the list on a computer and as its own page on a phone.

Narrow the list with **Source** (All, Task notes, Project notes, Standalone only, Unlinked), **Project** and **Label**. The filters are remembered on this device. On a phone they are behind **Filters**.

## Writing a note

**New note** (or `q` on the page) creates a standalone note, and so does typing a title in the search field and pressing <kbd>Enter</kbd> when no note matches. Give it a title, write in it with the same formatting as task notes (headings, lists, checklists, tables, links, code), and choose a project and labels below it if you like. A note without a title reads "Untitled note". Notes save on your device as you type and sync like everything else, offline included. On a computer, resize the note beside the list by its left edge: drag it, click it (widest, then back), or focus it and press ← or →; each device keeps its own width.

There are no folders: projects and labels organise notes. A note in a shared project is shared with that project: editors can change it and viewers can read it. A single note cannot be shared on its own. Moving a note into a project that is shared differently makes a copy there and removes the original, as for tasks.

Mentions, images and **Make subtask** are not offered in standalone notes: they need a task.

## Linking notes, tasks and projects

Type `[[` in a note to link to another note, a task or a project; what links to a note shows at its end. See [Link notes, tasks and projects](note-links.md). **Show connections** draws what an item is connected to, two steps out: [See an item's connections](note-connections.md).
