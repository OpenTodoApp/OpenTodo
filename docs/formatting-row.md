# The formatting row

<picture><source srcset="images/task-detail-dark.webp" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)"><img src="images/task-detail-light.webp" alt="The task panel with formatted notes under the title and the comments below."></picture>

The formatting row is a row of buttons above a note or a comment: **Bold**, **Italic**, **Link**, **Bullet list**, **Checklist** and **More**, which holds the rest of what the editor can do (see [Format notes and comments](formatting.md)). A button in the row keeps your selection, and you keep typing in the text after it.

## On a phone

The row sits above the text while you write, and goes away when you leave the text.

## On a computer

A small **Formatting** button (the bold **B**) sits at the top right corner of a task's or a project's note. It appears when you point at the note or move into it with the keyboard. Press it and the row stays above the note, whether you are typing in it or not; press it again to hide the row. The comment box has the same **Formatting** button next to its **@** button.

Whether the row is shown is remembered on each computer, separately for notes and for comments, so you can keep it in notes and leave comments plain. Notes you can only read have no button.

## From the keyboard

- <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> from a note reaches its **Formatting** button; <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> presses it.
- <kbd>Tab</kbd> then moves into the row, which is a single stop: <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> move between its buttons, <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> go to the first and the last, and <kbd>↓</kbd> opens **More**.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back to the text with your selection.

There is no shortcut for the button itself: every command has its own key or is in the `/` menu.

## Turn the button off

If you never want it, turn off **Formatting button** in **Settings → Account → Profile → [How you work](settings-you.md#how-you-work)**. It applies to all your devices. The shortcuts, the formatting you type and the `/` menu keep working, and phones keep their row. Turned on again, the button comes back as you left it.
