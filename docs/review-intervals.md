# Review intervals and maintenance

<picture><source srcset="images/review-walk-dark.webp" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)"><img src="images/review-walk-light.webp" alt="The Projects step of the weekly review: one card for the project Home renovation with its next action, and the walk's buttons with their keys."></picture>

With many projects, the [weekly review](review-and-statistics.md) gets long, and a project you only need to look at once a season costs as much attention as an active one. Two things keep it short: an interval per project, and Maintenance for projects you are deliberately keeping on low burn.

## How often a project comes up

Each project comes up in the walk once a week unless you choose otherwise. Choose **Review every…** in the project's menu, or press <kbd>e</kbd> on its card in the walk:

| Choice | The project is due again |
|---|---|
| **Every week** (the default) | 7 days after you last reviewed it |
| **Every 2 weeks** | 14 days after |
| **Every month** | on the same day a month later, or the month's last day when it is shorter (reviewed on 31 January, due on 28 February) |
| **Every quarter** | on the same day three months later |
| **Paused** | never; it only comes up when you choose **Review them too** |

A project you never reviewed is always due. Days are your days: with a day that [starts later than midnight](dates-and-times.md#when-a-day-ends), a review finished at 01:00 counts for the day before.

The choice is yours, like your mark of when you last reviewed it: in a shared project, each member keeps their own rhythm, and yours never reaches the others. It syncs to your own devices and works offline. Under [Simple lists](method/set-up.md), **Review every…** shows in the project menu only once you chose something other than every week; the walk card always offers it.

## Maintenance

Some projects are deliberately on low burn for a while: the garden in winter, the car between services. **Put in maintenance** in the project's menu, in its row menu on the [Projects page](all-projects.md), or <kbd>m</kbd> on its card in the walk, says so for everyone who shares it. While a project is in maintenance:
- its [health](all-projects.md#health) reads **Maintenance** instead of Overdue, Quiet or On track. **Late** (a passed deadline) still shows, because a deadline is usually a promise to someone else;
- it is never marked stalled or without a next action: not in the walk, not on the Loop home and not in the [weekly digest](statistics.md#weekly-digest-by-email);
- its tasks are listed, counted, dated and reminded as before, and its progress still shows;
- the project page reads **In maintenance** beside its name.

**End maintenance** in the same places turns it back. Each change offers Undo. In a shared project, the owner and admins can change it, like the project's colour. Under Simple lists the menu item shows only on a project that is in maintenance.
