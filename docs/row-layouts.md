# One-line and detailed rows

<picture><source srcset="images/row-layouts-dark.webp" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)"><img src="images/row-layouts-light.webp" alt="The Home renovation project with detailed rows: each task's title, then a line with its date and time, its priority as bars and P1 or P2, the estimate, the assignee and its labels."></picture>

Task rows come in two layouts:

- **One line** (the default): the title, with the date, project, labels, estimate and assignee in columns on the right that give way as the window narrows.
- **Detailed**: the title (two lines if it needs them), the parent task's name when the parent isn't shown just above, then one line with the date and time, the **priority** (bars and the letters P1, P2 or P3), the deadline, the start date, the project, the estimate and the assignee, and the labels: as many as fit the row, then **+2** for the rest. Hover or focus the labels to read every name. On a phone or with a large text size the line wraps, so nothing is cut off.

The date stays on the row in both layouts. Priority is never shown by colour alone: a one-line row shows the bars (three for priority 1, two for priority 2, one for priority 3) after the title, and screen readers read "Priority 1".

## For every list

**Settings › Appearance › Theme and density › Task rows** picks the layout of every list: Today, Upcoming, the Inbox, search results and every view, project and label without its own choice. It syncs to your devices.

## For one view, project or label

**Display options** (the sliders button in the header) of a saved view, a project's list or a label's page has **One line** and **Detailed** at the top. The choice is kept for that view, project or label on all your devices and wins over the setting. See [Group and order a list](group-and-order.md) and [Saved views](saved-views.md).
