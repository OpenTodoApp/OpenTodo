# Code of conduct

OpenTodo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep discussions about the work, and make room for people who are new. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, discussions, pull requests or any other project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, email **raffaele.pizzari@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
