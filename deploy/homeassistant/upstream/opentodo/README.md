# Home Assistant add-on: OpenTodo

_Open-source, local-first task manager. Works offline and syncs every device from one small container._

![Supports aarch64 Architecture][aarch64-shield]
![Supports amd64 Architecture][amd64-shield]

Runs [OpenTodo](https://open-todo.app) on Home Assistant OS or a Supervised installation: tasks, projects, recurring tasks, reminders, sharing with your household, CalDAV, and apps for every device from the browser. Your tasks stay on your Home Assistant machine and are included in its backups.

To see and change your tasks from Home Assistant itself (to-do lists, automations), add the [OpenTodo integration](https://open-todo.app/docs/home-assistant/) too.
