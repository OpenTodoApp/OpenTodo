Description:
OpenTodo is an open-source task manager you host yourself. This app opens your OpenTodo server and adds what only a phone app can do:

- Reminders on time, scheduled on the phone, with no push service in between
- Share links, text, photos and files from any app into a new task
- A Today widget on the home screen
- Unlock an end-to-end encrypted account with Face ID or Touch ID

You need your own OpenTodo server: enter its address on the first screen. The self-hosting guide is on the project's website, and demo.open-todo.app lets you try it without an account.

The app needs a connection to your server to start. For offline use, add OpenTodo to the home screen from Safari instead.

No trackers, no analytics, no ads. The app talks only to the server you enter.
